# 0.6.3 draft — a darker room, and lamps that read as reflections

User prompt for this round:

```
this decor lamps and overall scene global illumination not good looks (i prefer more
dark as in basic style mode, and new lamps seems make all brighter and looks not so
interesting). try to change design. maybe less visible and more detailed reflection
```

Reading:

- The glass skin is *additive* where it should be *material*. Everything it bakes —
  the board-wide veil, the floor grain, the glaze over the paint, the lamp haloes —
  hands light back to the scene, so `fx=glass` sits on a grey, hazy board while the
  plain renderer keeps the scheme's black. The complaint is about global luminance,
  not about one sprite.
- The five lamp reflections are 5–7 cm wide bright bars with a 4× vertical halo. They
  are the brightest thing on the board, they read as white smudges at 1:1, and the
  additive bar greys whatever teal or blue it crosses.
- The ask is the opposite of a bigger lamp: **less visible, more detailed**. Detail in
  a reflection is structure — a fixture's body, a filament, ribs, cuts, a colour
  fringe — and structure is bought with *contrast*, not with light. So the design
  should spend its contrast on a narrow tube with a dark seat around it and give the
  rest of the board back to the scheme.

KeRot — rotating disk curling

Drag on the board to set a stroke (direction + power, 0–3 m/s), then throw with Space. Hold shift while dragging for fine speed control; arrow keys nudge aim (shift = fine there too), Esc clears the stroke, R resets. Pucks the disk can hold (inside the dashed eject ring) ride it around and pick up its spin — the 6-sector face shows each puck's rotation — the rest are flung onto the cold floor. Every seat gets 4 pucks and pushes one per turn. From the push, each seat earns dt × the red area of the big disk its pucks cover while the board resolves; the red field rotates with the disk. The shaded eject fan and wedge + lane rule return spent pucks to their owner's stock. The two half-circles above and below the disk are black holes: entering one permanently removes a puck. Try the retriever, black-hole, or balanced bots to compare recovery and attack strategies.

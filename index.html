KeRot — rotating disk curling

Drag on the board to set a stroke (direction + power), then throw with Space. Drag again or press Esc to change your mind. Arrow keys nudge aim, R resets. Pucks the disk can hold (inside the dashed eject ring) ride it around; the rest are flung onto the cold floor. Recycled pucks return to the pool for reuse; parked pucks score at the end.

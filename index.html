KeRot — rotating disk curling

Every seat gets 4 pucks and pushes one per turn. Pucks the disk can hold (inside the dashed eject ring) ride it around and pick up its spin — the 6-sector face shows each puck's rotation — the rest are flung onto the cold floor.

controls

  • drag on the board to set a stroke: direction + power, 0–3 m/s
  • Space throws · Esc clears the stroke · R resets the board
  • hold shift while dragging for fine speed control
  • ← → nudge the aim, ↑ ↓ the speed (shift = fine there too)
  • swipe in from the left edge for this panel

score

From the push, each seat earns dt × the red area of the big disk its pucks cover while the board resolves; the red field rotates with the disk. Scores are shown normalised to 0–999 per game, so the number means the same thing for 2 seats and for 8.

"Red" is the red channel of the paint: blue pays about a fifth, pale and red pay nearly the same — the legend under disk pattern has the numbers.

disk pattern

soft lobes (the original), 3 red circles of different sizes, or 6 rim hemispheres — the richest red sits at the rim, right where pucks are flung off. The pattern is the score map, so it can be switched mid-game.

puck mass

Ice grips a heavy puck less: friction falls as mass−1/3. Heavier pucks slide farther and are flung off sooner — the eject ring shrinks — while light ones stop short and stick. auto-ω re-solves the 90 % rule for the current mass. The slider acts on every puck on the board.

return pucks

The shaded eject fan and the wedge + lane rule return spent pucks to their owner's stock — pick one in the side panel.

black holes

The two half-circles above and below the disk are black holes: entering one permanently removes a puck.

bots

Try the retriever, black-hole, or balanced bots to compare recovery and attack strategies. A seat can be switched between human and bot at any time.

colour scheme

classic, or kerot-s: navy ice and cream pucks whose centre gauge and tag show the share of the best possible rate each puck is earning right now.

presets and links

The preset select applies a named bundle of settings — physics, disk pattern, return rule, colour scheme, the table itself — and starts a fresh board with it. Touch any control afterwards and you have left the bundle, and the select clears. Picking a preset also puts its link into the address bar, so what you see is what you can share.

The same settings are URL parameters: ?preset=stones, or by hand ?schema=rim6&seats=human,hunter,keeper&mass=8&omega=1.2. A named preset applies first and the rest override it, and the link is kept as you typed it — including those overrides — so a refresh opens the same board. Touch a control and the address bar switches to the settings that differ from the shipped table, which is the link of what you see now. preset, omega, muk, mus, mass (kg), schema (classic | circles | rim6), recycle (none | zones | advance), theme (classic | kerot), seats (a comma list of seat types), balls, ring, ghost (0|1). Names are case-insensitive. Unknown keys are ignored and numbers are clamped to the slider ranges — a hand-typed link always opens.

KeRot — rotating disk curling

Every seat gets 4 pucks and pushes one per turn. Pucks the disk can hold (inside the dashed eject ring) ride it around and pick up its spin — the 6-sector face shows each puck's rotation — the rest are flung onto the cold floor.

controls

  • drag on the board to set a stroke: direction + power, 0–3 m/s
  • Space throws · Esc clears the stroke · R resets the board
  • hold shift while dragging for fine speed control
  • ← → nudge the aim, ↑ ↓ the speed (shift = fine there too)
  • swipe in from the left edge for this panel

score

From the push, each seat earns dt × the red area of the big disk its pucks cover while the board resolves; the red field rotates with the disk. Scores are shown normalised to 0–999 per game, so the number means the same thing for 2 seats and for 8.

return pucks

The shaded eject fan and the wedge + lane rule return spent pucks to their owner's stock — pick one in the side panel.

black holes

The two half-circles above and below the disk are black holes: entering one permanently removes a puck.

bots

Try the retriever, black-hole, or balanced bots to compare recovery and attack strategies. A seat can be switched between human and bot at any time.

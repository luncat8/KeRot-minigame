# 0.6.7 draft — 3-rounded: outline by the three circles alone, 6 cm gap

User prompt for this round:

```
in the mode

it remains leftovers from parent shape: return zone parts corresponding to holes are not cut as increasing holes circles but some curved strange outlines. it should be outlined just by circular shapes (2 from holes and 1 from big disk). also sizes make smaller to smaller clear ice gaps by ~30%. he call it as "8 cm" so try something like 6
```

Notes:

- "Leftovers from parent shape" are the wedge's straight sides (corner → disk
  centre). Near each hole the side meets the hole's cut circle and the outline
  spikes into a point; the inset lines follow the same spike. The holes lie
  outside the wedge, so their circles only nick it.
- "Outlined just by circular shapes (2 from holes and 1 from big disk)" means
  no straight edge may reach the outline: the wedge sides must be covered by
  the cut circles themselves.
- The clear-ice gap was 8 cm (`ZONE_CUT_GAP`); try 6 cm.

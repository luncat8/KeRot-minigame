# 0.2 worklog — rotation-aware bots, recovery, and black holes

Reference: `0.2-draft-improve-bot.md`; implementation plan: `archive/0.2-plan.md`.

## Review of 0.1.6

Fetched and inspected commit `e87ca935a239a2e97c79f3b25a86fa8c0ee83968` (0.1.6). Its source tree is the base for this checkout, with the 0.2 draft added afterward. The live roster-removal path had a real ownership bug: it removed a seat's pucks and spliced the roster, but surviving pucks and permanent-loss counters above that index kept their old owner index. A later score tick could dereference a missing seat. `Game.removePlayer()` now removes the departing pucks, reindexes owners/counters, then splices; smoke coverage verifies that a surviving puck keeps its identity, scores, and retains its owner's loss counter.

## Implementation

- Added two visible semicircular black holes on the upper/lower left boundary. Physics uses swept circle-entry time-of-impact tests, so a fast grazing puck cannot skip a hole between fixed steps. A captured puck is swap-removed permanently, is not returned to reserve, and increments its owner's loss count; the HUD reports the total.
- Added stable puck UIDs and per-seat loss counters to physics state and predictor cloning. Predictions now keep following their candidate when another puck is captured and swap-removed.
- Added one-pass per-seat score rates and 30 Hz trapezoidal score integration over the candidate turn while `theta` advances. Candidate values include own integrated score, future score opportunity, opponent denial, recoveries under the active return rule, reserve scarcity, own-loss risk, and opponent captures.
- Replaced the separate bot heuristics with a common 16-stroke candidate search biased toward the disk center, rotating hot lobe, own off-disk pucks, and enemies nearest a hole. Added `retriever`, `blackhole`, and `balanced`; all bot profiles keep nonzero scoring, recovery, reserve, and risk terms. The `retriever` profile is deliberately recovery-heavy without zeroing its scoring objective.
- Updated player selectors, help text, hole rendering, the permanent-loss readout, and headless callers to pass reserve context. Added `experiments/bot-strategies.js` and kept its report separate from the 0.1.6 competition results.
- Adjusted the energy fixture's starting point: its old location was inside the newly added lower black hole, so the puck was correctly captured before the stopping-distance assertion.

The retriever returned over twice as many balls as the highest-return non-retriever and remained competitive on score. The black-hole profile eliminated the most opponents and traded away scoring as intended; the balanced profile had the best win rate in this seeded run while staying near the top scores. Twenty games are directional, not a statistically conclusive tournament. The comparison averaged about 35 ms per bot decision.

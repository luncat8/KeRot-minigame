# 0.4 worklog — kerot-s review, mass slider, disk schemas, UI colour schemes

Reference: `0.4-draft.md` (this round's user prompt and its reading).

## Review of 0.3

- **Implicit globals in `bakeZone`** (`js/render.js`): `l1, l2, a0, a1` were assigned
  without a declaration, so every bake leaked them onto `window` — and a strict-mode
  evaluation throws `ReferenceError: l1 is not defined`. Fixed with `const`.
  `experiments/client-smoke.js` now evaluates every browser file in strict mode; run on
  HEAD `46cc337`, it fails exactly there.
- **The floor carried a frozen copy of the field**: `texFloor`, the whole board baked at
  θ = 0 and drawn at 35 % alpha, so a blue-and-red halo around the disk stood still while
  the disk turned under it — wrong a second after the first throw. The floor is now plain
  ice (classic tint `#49586f` = the old far-field colour, so the look holds).
- **`compete.js` was not re-run in 0.3.** The last row of `results/history.csv` predates
  0.3 (hunter 676, keeper 426, random 109); HEAD reproduces 246 / 241 / 274 — measured on
  a worktree of `46cc337`, identical to this round's run except for think time. Since
  0.3, random outscores both scoring bots in the 3-seat competition.
- **Rim straddlers were mis-paid.** The score averaged the friction pattern's cells that
  land on the disk; a sliver over the rim got no cell and earned nothing, and a
  half-covered puck kept a dozen cells. Measured against a fine quadrature (exact lens
  area × fine mean): classic straddlers up to 1.3 % off (1.0 % of a full puck on slivers),
  and on a paint whose colour edges meet the rim — the rim schema of this round — up to
  6.5 % off. Fixed (below).
- Per-frame string allocations: `drawGhost` built six `rgba(…)` strings per frame and the
  pucks a `String(i + 1)` each; both are tables now.

## Review of kerot-s (`luncat8/kerot-s`, `a60c50e`)

Ideas taken:

- **A mass slider** — rebuilt on a load law, because kerot-s's does nothing (bug 1).
- **The 3-circle field** (zones in units of R with a distance ramp) → schema `circles`.
- **The puck**: cream body on a drop shadow, two shaded quarters of a rim band glued to
  the puck so its turning shows, a per-puck mark offset so pucks never line up, a centre
  gauge and a value tag → the puck of the `kerot-s` UI scheme.
- **The panel look** (navy, monospace, amber accent, blue range thumbs), **the floor**
  (vertical gradient + faint 8 × 4 grid) and **the colour legend** → scheme + legend.

Already here: the cone-limit readout (`d_hold/R`) and geometry derived from requirements
(auto-ω, the 90 % rule). Not taken: its spin, wall and on-disk models (bugs 3–5).

Bugs found:

1. **The mass slider is a live no-op.** `ui.js` `syncSliders` sets `C.PUCK_M` *and* every
   `p.m` to the slider value, so in `sim.js` `load = (C.PUCK_M / p.m)^(1/3) ≡ 1` and
   `tau = SPIN_TAU · p.m / C.PUCK_M ≡ 1`; equal masses cancel in impacts. Only its
   experiments, which set masses directly, see an effect.
2. **"A heavier puck holds a wider cone" is backwards**: a lower effective μs *shrinks*
   the hold radius μs·g/ω²; the cone readout and its dashed ring ignore the load factor.
3. **The spin model is unphysical**: spin relaxes toward the surface vorticity — on bare
   ice 2.58 of 3 rad/s remain after 1.5 s and 0.13 rad/s after 30 s, where patch friction
   stops it in ~0.05 s. "Spin-independent friction" does not hold for a finite patch.
4. **`onDisk` is all-or-nothing**: the full disk velocity applies at the puck centre as
   soon as the patch edge touches the disk, even with the centre off it.
5. **Walls are a position clamp after the move** (no time of impact), and `impact()`
   teleports `b` by `rr` when two centres coincide.
6. **The palette's red channel is non-monotone** (mid 234 > red 204): under red-channel
   scoring, pale would pay more than red.
7. Minor: the hand puck is drawn over the thrown one; the throw sweep passes an unused
   `S.layout(0.5)` argument; `.gitignore.txt` is misnamed and therefore inert.

## Implementation

- **`js/cfg.js`** — `massRef` 4, `massMin` 0.5, `massMax` 20, `diskSchema` 'classic'.
  `loadFactor(cfg, m) = ∛(massRef/m)`; `muKEff`, `muSEff`, `stopDistance`; `holdRadius`
  and `autoOmega` use the effective μs. `FIELD_SOURCES`/`FIELD_BIAS` moved into
  `js/field.js` as the classic schema.
- **`js/physics.js`** — friction, the ride test and the lock test scale with the puck's
  own load factor; the slider mass's factor is taken once per step (a `cbrt` per puck
  cost 15 % of the step). `holdable` only sees μk/μs, so the factor cancels there.
- **`js/field.js`** — `DISK_SCHEMAS`: `classic` ('soft lobes', the old Gaussians),
  `circles` ('3 red circles', kerot-s's zones, y flipped), `rim6` ('6 rim hemispheres',
  r 0.42 R, ramp 0.16 R; leaves 0.16 R blue gaps on the rim). Gaussian sums (`blobField`)
  or the strongest circle distance ramp (`ringField`, circles merge without adding up).
  Each schema lists `aims` for the bots — on `rim6` at 0.78 R, inside the hold radius.
  One palette for all, its red channel rising with v (blue 22 %, pale 96 %, red 100 %).
  `bakeFieldCanvas(box, ppm, cfg)` bakes disk-local boxes only.
- **`js/score.js`** — one red LUT per schema, at 128 cells (1.25 cm) instead of 96: finer
  only sharpens the edges the whole-puck pattern then aliases (measured — 160 and 192
  made the ring schemas *worse*). A puck over the rim is integrated on its own terms
  (`lensRed`): rays from the puck centre clipped to the disk exactly, 4 Gauss points
  along each (weight t), 16 Gauss points in angle over each of the two arcs between the
  points where the circles cross — inside an arc the clipped length is smooth, which is
  what Gauss needs. A fixed fan of rays converges slowly through those crossings (32
  rays 2.4 %, 128 rays 0.4 % on `rim6`). `RED_MAX` is exported.
- **`js/bots.js`** — target group 1 is the schema aim nearest the puck at the current θ
  (classic has one aim, so classic play is unchanged).
- **`js/render.js`** — a theme table (`classic`, `kerot`): background, floor (flat or
  gradient + grid), walls, rim, eject ring, spin tick, HUD cells, ink, font, puck drawer.
  `drawPuckKerot` is the kerot-s puck with the owner's seat colour as its ring; its
  gauge and tag show `puckRate / (π r² · RED_MAX)`, the share of the best possible rate,
  and the tag is outlined so it reads over pale paint too. The disk sprite is rebaked
  when `CFG.diskSchema` changes; fonts and ghost inks are rebuilt on a scheme change
  only. The aim label uses `stopDistance`.
- **`js/game.js`** — `setPuckMass(m)` sets `CFG.puckM` and every puck on the board;
  `setDiskSchema(name)` sets the schema and makes a bot that is aiming re-aim.
- **`js/ui.js` / `index.html`** — a disk-pattern select with a legend generated from
  `fieldColor` (bar + what each anchor pays), a log10 mass slider (readout
  "4.00 kg · μ ×1.00", and the μ readouts show "0.030 → 0.024" when mass rescales them),
  a colour-scheme select setting `body[data-theme]`. The stylesheet is 21 custom
  properties with a `body[data-theme=kerot]` override block; the classic values are
  the old literals, borders included, so classic is unchanged. Help text: score,
  disk pattern, puck mass, colour scheme.
- **Experiments** — `mass-cancellation.js` → `mass-load.js` (the old invariant is now
  deliberately false); `field-preview.js` covers every schema; `scoring.js` checks every
  schema plus 120 random rim straddlers each; `bot-strategies.js [games] [seed] [schema]`;
  `client-smoke.js` covers both schemes × all schemas and the three new controls;
  `render-frames.js` (new) writes real PNG frames per scheme × schema through
  `@napi-rs/canvas` (`experiments/logs/frames`, git-ignored).

## Decisions taken without asking

- Mass is global and live (every puck, the board included) on a log slider 0.5–20 kg; the
  μ sliders keep meaning "the coefficient of a 4 kg puck".
- `rim6`: r 0.42 R / ramp 0.16 R, phase 0 (a hemisphere on +x). A puck centred 0.70 R out
  on an axis earns 87 % of the maximum, at 0.80 R 100 %; the gaps stay blue (22–25 %).
- The UI scheme never changes the disk paint (it is the score), and kerot-s keeps a green
  throw button (0.3's request), muted to its palette.

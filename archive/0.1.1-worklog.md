## Design corrections made during implementation (folded into the plan)

1. **The obvious zone design collapsed into the behind rule.** A "channel +
   crescent around the disk" (channel x<0.6, left half-annulus 0.92–1.35) covers
   essentially the same recyclable set as `x < diskX` minus the disk — the two
   radio options would have played ~95 % the same. A death-map measurement (40
   random-bot games, one sample per puck) showed deaths split into the left shelf
   (49 %, mostly x<0.6) and the top/bottom wall bands (46 %, y<0.25/1.95). The
   final zones are board gutters (lagoon x<0.60 + streams y<0.22/1.98, ~28 % of the
   board — measured bands beat the draft's 20 % guess) which catch 98 % of dead
   pucks and are visibly a different rule from the behind-half.
2. Zone hit-tests and the behind rule share an `onDisk` guard (dist ≤ R + r): the
   lagoon runs under the disk's left cap and must never recycle a riding/scoring
   puck.
3. `maxThrows = 48` is a real rule (game + competition): with recycling on, a
   player/bot that only ever recycles would otherwise loop forever.

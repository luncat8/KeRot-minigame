# 0.5.8 worklog — review pass

Scope: the prompt's own review pass (no bot-balance work). See `0.5.8-draft.md` for the
reading and the decisions. All edits are on the current branch; no commit was needed to
keep them.

## Found and fixed

1. **The Snowfall adapter kept animating after the result.** `minigame/entry.js`
   `frame()` called `schedule()` unconditionally after `finish()`. Once `g.answer()` had
   resolved the story slot, the host may replace the mount's markup, but the game kept a
   `requestAnimationFrame` chain, stepping physics and drawing into a detached canvas for
   as long as the scene stayed open. `schedule()` now refuses to schedule once
   `finished` is set; pause, visibility and intersection transitions go through the same
   guard, so none of them can restart a resolved match. The final frame is drawn before
   `finish()`, and the host's `cleanup()` still removes listeners as before.

2. **The kerot-s floor gradient went stale on a board refit.** `js/render.js` built the
   theme's linear gradient once in `setup()` at `cfg.boardH`. A fit-view resize changes
   that height (`Game.fitBoard` and `Render.resize(true)` are paired in `js/main.js`),
   while `resize()` only rebuilt zone paths, so the lower part of the ice kept the old
   gradient's end colour. Gradients now live in a per-renderer `floorFill` map and are
   rebuilt alongside the zone paths on a geometry change. This also stops two renderer
   instances (page + story slot) from sharing one theme object's gradient.

3. **Score LUT cache key.** `js/score.js` keyed its baked red grid — and the mean used
   by the bots' ball unit — by schema alone, though the grid is sampled in disk-local
   metres and its scale is `diskR`. The key is now `schema@radius`; values at the shipped
   radius are unchanged.

4. **The panel promised a turn that may not come.** `stateText()` said "next seat in N s"
   for the whole resolve window, including the last throw. With a return rule on, a
   recycled ball can bring a seat back, so a reliable "last throw" label needs a
   look-ahead the panel does not have. The line now says "scoring window closes in N s",
   which is true in every resolve.

5. **Instructions lagged the 0.5.6 gesture.** The help drawer and `README.md` still said
   to drag from the puck; the input aims from the ice outside it and throws on a tap of
   the puck. Both now say that.

  line names its window and never says "next seat".

  then calls cleanup and checks that pointer/viewport listeners were removed.

  `experiments/mass-load.js`, `experiments/bench.js`: all pass; physics remains ~0.08 ms
  per 60 Hz frame on the bench board.
- `node --check` over every `js/`, `minigame/`, `tools/` and `experiments/` script, plus
  `git diff --check`: clean.
- Extra review harness (scratch, not committed): 20 000 simulated frames with live seat
  edits, speed rules, votes, mass/schema/fit changes, pause and resets — no NaN state,
  owner index or pending puck out of range, and no puck above `MAX_PUCKS`.

  failing gate were reverted, not papered over.

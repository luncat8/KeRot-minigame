# 0.6.1 plan — ice as a material: paint under it, lamps in it, colour in the stones

## Goal

Make the glass option read as a sheet of ice over a paint layer, and make a stone's
colour audible. Three changes, all in `js/fx-glass.js` apart from one renderer hook and
docs:

1. field markings look painted under the ice,
2. ceiling lamps are mirrored, blurrily, in the ice in a few places,
3. each seat colour rings at its own note.

Everything stays opt-in and cache-only: the add-on bakes fixed sprites once and each
frame draws them. No physics, scoring, bot or determinism change.

## Architecture

- Add a sixth no-op hook, `skin.surface(ctx, view, cfg)`, to `js/render.js`. It runs
  after the disk, its rim and the eject ring, before the black holes, the launch box,
  the ghost, the aim and the stones, inside the board clip. It therefore sits over every
  marking on the ice and under everything that is not paint. `js/render.js` changes by
  one guarded call; the plain page pays that check only.
- `js/fx-glass.js` owns the look and the sound. Its bakes stay fixed-size so a window
  resize costs transform work, never a rebake:
  - `glazeTex` (512²) — the ice thickness over the disk: milk that thickens toward the
    rim, frost patches, and trapped air (dark rim, bright core).
  - `milkTex` (432×264) — the same veil over the whole board, so the zone hatch and the
    launch box lose their vector sharpness too.
  - `lampTex[0..1]` (432×264) — ceiling lamps mirrored in the ice, in board space.
  - `softTex` — a scattered copy of the disk schema, baked once per schema change.
- The baked sizes are board-proportional and scaled by the draw; reflections and frost
  are blur by definition, so bilinear scaling is free extra softness.

## Under-ice paint

- Paint under ice is not sharp: it is scattered. `softTex` is the shipped field painter
  (`bakeFieldCanvas`, `js/field.js`) run at 60 px/m and drawn over the sharp paint at
  0.76 (classic) / 0.82 (kerot) alpha inside the rotating frame. It is the *same* field, so it can only soften
  edges — never move a boundary, and never change the red channel the score reads.
- `glazeTex` goes on top: a cool milk that is thinner at the worn centre and thicker at
  the cold rim, frost patches, and 150 trapped-air bubbles. Then the existing brushed
  polish (`diskTex`) stays above that, because the scratches belong to the surface.
- `milkTex` covers the rest of the board at roughly half that strength, with fine pit
  marks, so the zone paint is under the same ice as the disk paint.
- The wash is deliberately bounded: the average disk luminance rises ~10 levels, enough
  to read as depth, not enough to confuse the score map or the seat rings. Markings,
  numbers, gauges, seat rings, the eject ring, the voids and the HUD are untouched.

## Lamp reflections

- A reflection belongs to the surface, not to the disk, so the lamps are baked in board
  metres and never rotate with `w.theta`. Five lamps in two groups, placed off the HUD,
  the zone labels and the launch lane.
- Each is built as a real reflection seen at a low angle: an upright elliptical halo,
  a soft vertical smear along the line of sight, then the tube as a bright bar with two
  bloom passes.
- Drawn additively (`lighter`) so a bar brightens the teal of a zone instead of turning
  it grey. The two groups breathe on separate slow phases (~0.37 and 0.48 Hz, ±10 %) so
  the ice is not a sticker; under `prefers-reduced-motion` they hold mid brightness.

## Per-colour voices

- `VOICES[8]` mirrors `SEAT_COLORS` order (`js/players.js`): a pentatonic pitch ladder
  (1.00 … 1.90), a partial spread per seat and a decay that shortens as the note rises,
  so red knocks low and warm while blue rings high and thin.
- `glassPing` takes pitch, spread and decay instead of a fixed pair of constants. Rail
  taps use the stone's own voice; a contact between two colours rings at the geometric
  mean of their pitches with the spread of the pair — a note neither stone makes alone.
- `stoneDetune(uid)` adds ±1.2 % per stone so same-colour pucks are not a unison.
  Still three oscillators per contact, still rate-limited, still derived from velocity
  jumps in `frame()` — no event hooks in physics.

## Validation

- `node experiments/fx-smoke.js`: the surface hook draws the veil plus two lamp groups,
  the disk stacks scatter + glaze + polish, the scattered paint bakes once and rebakes
  only on a schema change, a rail tap changes pitch with the seat, and a two-colour
  contact rings between the two voices.
- `node experiments/client-smoke.js`: six hooks reachable, `surface` once per frame.
- `node tools/build-minigame.js && --check`, `node experiments/minigame-smoke.js`, the
  physics/scoring/vote gates, `node experiments/render-frames.js --glass [outDir]` for
  real PNGs, and a software-canvas timing check for the added draws.

## Rejected

- `ctx.filter = 'blur()'` per frame: repaints the disk every frame instead of compositing
  one baked sprite.
- Blurring the paint by shrinking the *score* texture: the score map must stay exact.
- A lamp reflection that rotates with the disk: lamps are in the room, not on the plate.
- Static (non-additive) reflection sprites: they turn grey over the teal zone paint.
- Splitting the reflection into one texture per lamp: five draws instead of two for a
  difference nobody can see, and no way to breathe them out of phase.
- A held note per seat in the ice hiss: the hiss follows total sliding speed, and colour
  in it would fight the contact sounds that carry the colour.

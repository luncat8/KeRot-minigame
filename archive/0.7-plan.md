# 0.7 plan — return zone markings and hole shapes

Draft: 0.7-draft.md. Design drafts in archive/temp/ (3-rounded-score, 6-hemis,
default-wavy-lines) plus the user's captions are the spec.

Two new option groups, separate selectors, both live in the side panel and in the
URL, like the disk pattern:

## zoneStyle — the return zone marking (CFG.zoneStyle)

- `straight` (default): 45° diagonal hatch fill + the wedge outline — the shipped look.
- `wavy`: the fill becomes horizontal wavy lines (waves flow across the zone);
  outline unchanged.
- `arc`: the outline becomes a single big circular arc — the zone's far boundary as
  part of a very big circle, bulging toward the disk; the fill stays the diagonal
  hatch. The rule geometry (the zone tables) is untouched: the arc is a marking.

## holeShape — the black holes (CFG.holeShape)

- `flush` (default): the voids stay centred on the board edges, the wall cuts them
  to half-discs — the shipped look.
- `rounded`: the voids become full circles inset from the edges (`holePositions`
  moves the centres in by r). The zone is cut around the holes **and** the big
  disk with a bigger outline (guard + ZONE_GAP) leaving a good gap between the
  marking and the voids/disk.
- `hemis`: half-disc domes against the edges — flat side on the wall, bulging into
  play, drawn with a dome gradient (lit from above).

## plumbing

- js/cfg.js: the two CFG keys, `holePositions(cfg)` (shared by render, physics,
  bots, glass clip and the board refit in game.js).
- js/render.js: wavy hatch path, arc outline paths, hole cutouts in bakeZone,
  hole-body branches in drawBlackHoles.
- js/presets.js: default bundle keys, applyCfg, `?zone=`, `?hole=`.
- js/ui.js: selects, handlers (CFG + Render.resize(true) to rebake the zone
  paths), URL echo, SHIPPED table.
- index.html: zone marking + hole shape selects on one row directly under the
  `view` row at the top of the panel (so the new design settings are visible
  without digging), help text.
- games/kerot.js: regenerate with tools/build-minigame.js.

## validation

- experiments/zone-hole-styles.js renders every (zoneStyle, holeShape) combo to
  PNG and prints a pixel sanity check; ASCII-checked against the drafts.
- experiments/render-frames.js, client-smoke.js, minigame-smoke.js must stay green.

## results (done)

- All nine zoneStyle × holeShape combos render; pairwise diff masks confirm each
  style changes the frame exactly as intended (arc = the big circular outline,
  wavy = the hatch fill, rounded = the inset full-circle voids with the zone cut
  around them, hemis = the lit edge domes) — see experiments/logs/zone-hole/.
- The launch-lane rect outline is stroked outside the clip so it stays complete
  in every style.
- client-smoke.js extended: the two selects list every option, picks apply, the
  address bar echoes `zone=`/`hole=`, and the default preset restores
  straight/flush. Green.
- minigame-smoke.js green (the earlier crash was a stale bundle, not a regression).
- zone-catch.js and the render-frames tone gate fail identically on the pristine
  tree — pre-existing, unrelated to this feature.

# 0.5.10 — armed puck identity, spent-board stock, 4 s end brake

Scope: the three play reports in `0.6-draft.md` — (1) a throw that shows no ghost and then
does nothing, (2) a ball coming back after the game ended, (3) a slower brake at the end of
a game.

## 1. No ghost, and the ball does nothing

**Reproduced first.** A headless run of the real flow (4 bot seats × 8 balls, 60 fps
frames, `tick`/`beforeSteps`/`stepWorld`/`afterSteps`) reached SCORED in ~12k frames and hit
the state **twice** per game: `state === 'aim'` while `w.uid[pending]` no longer matched the
uid the puck was armed with. A second trace pinned the shape: the armed puck was spawned at
`n-1`, a puck at a lower index was swallowed by a black hole, `swapRemovePuck` copied the
armed puck into the freed slot, and `pending` kept pointing at the slot it left.

The two symptoms the prompt describes are the two readers of that index:

- the ghost (`predictStroke(pred, w, pending, …)`) tracked the *copy* of the armed puck that
  the removal left behind — asleep at the launch spot — so `pred.n` was 1 and the preview
  drew nothing (the renderer skips `n < 2`);
- the throw (`throwPuck(w, pending, …)`) set `vx/vy/st` on an entry `>= w.n`, which the step
  loop never visits, so the ball never moved, never scored, and was claimed by the return
  rule at the handoff — "the ball just disappears".

Fixed in `js/game.js`:

- `pendingUid` remembers the armed puck's uid (set in `armTurn`);
- `syncPending()` re-finds the puck by uid when `w.uid[pending]` disagrees, updates
  `aim.pending` and re-sims the ghost (the puck that was in the way is gone); if no slot
  holds the uid it hands the ball back and re-arms, so a lost puck cannot stall a turn;
- called at the top of `tick`, `beforeSteps`, `afterSteps` and `commitThrow` — the armed
  puck is current before anything reads it, and the common case is one compare.
- `findPuckUid` is now exported by `js/predict.js` (it already existed for the predictor's
  own tracking); the browser build sees it as a global either way.

A new identity needs to travel with every *copy* of the flow: the first version restored
`pending` from the time-warp snapshot without its uid, so the planner's copy re-armed the
turn it had just loaded. `experiments/time-warp.js` caught it (`FAIL the copy plays the turn
the game plays`, 4 failures, t 3.62 s against the live game's 4.02 s); `saveFlow`/`loadFlow`
now carry `pendingUid` (and `js/warp.js`'s record declares it), and all gates pass again.

This is the same lesson `findings-pitfalls-skills.md` already records for the predictor —
"an array index is not an identity" — and the note is extended with the game-flow instance.

## 2. A ball came back after the game was over

The return rule ran at the `settle -> scored` transition with `giveBack`, while the 4 s
brake was still shedding riders. A puck that came to rest in the return zone after the last
throw therefore put a ball back into a stock that has no turn left to spend it.

`afterSteps`: the spent board still calls `recyclePucks` (the zone is the board's rule and the
final picture keeps it) but passes `null` for the callback, so nothing is credited. The
`handOff` path is untouched: a return inside a turn window still brings a seat back, which is
what keeps a round alive.

## 3. The end brake is 4 s

- `js/cfg.js`: `settleTime` 3 -> 4 s, with the note that the end of a game is a brake
  (`beginSettle` ramps the motor to 0 over it, so a linear spin-down, not a freeze).
  Measured from the default 0.7831 rad/s: 0.734 at 0.25 s, 0.587 at 1 s, 0.392 at 2 s,
  0.196 at 3 s, 0 at 4.0 s, `settled` true there.
- `minigame/entry.js`: `MAX_SETTLE` and the fallback are now `BASE_CFG.settleTime`, so the
  embedded match brakes over the same 4 s and a host may still ask for a shorter stop
  (floor 0.25 s). `games/kerot.js` rebuilt (`node tools/build-minigame.js`).

## Validation

- `experiments/client-smoke.js` — new gates, both verified to fail without their fix:
  - armed-puck identity: after a removal mid-aim, `pending` follows the uid, the ghost has
    more than 4 samples and leaves the launch spot, the throw reaches the puck and the puck
    moves. With `syncPending` removed the same block reports
    `FAIL the ghost still leaves the launch spot — 1 samples, 0.000 m` and the throw/puck
    checks fail, i.e. the reported bug exactly;
  - spent board: a sleeping puck planted in the return zone during the brake is still
    cleared at SCORED, and the seat's stock stays 0. With `giveBack` restored the block
    reports `FAIL a spent board returns no balls — 1`.
- `experiments/minigame-smoke.js` — bundle matches its sources, live match, cleanup, saved
  and skip results pass.
- `experiments/settings-revive.js` — full round to SCORED, over-timer handoffs, spent board
  restart: unchanged.
- `experiments/time-warp.js` — "the copy plays the turn the game plays" and the search
  bounds: all gates passed (it is what found the missing snapshot field, above).
- `experiments/speed-vote.js` (motor ramps, spin-down hold radius), `determinism.js`,
  `zone-catch.js`, `scoring.js`, `spin.js` — all OK.

## Next step

- Worth measuring whether the 4 s brake should shed riders gradually
  (`speed-vote.js` already prints the hold radius along a ramp) — a rider released at
  ~0.69 m still travels noticeably during the stop.
- The armed puck is now tracked by uid everywhere it is read; the remaining index-based
  readers are `aim.pending` (kept in step) and the renderer, which draws whichever slot the
  game names — a renderer that took the uid would make the family closed by construction.

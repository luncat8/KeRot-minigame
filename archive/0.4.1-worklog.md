## Review of the previous pass

The previous pass landed the preset select, `js/presets.js`, and a rim-feed candidate group, and stopped without a worklog. Three things in that pass were wrong or unfinished.

- **A boot link was rewritten down to `?preset=name`.** `?preset=stones&omega=2` applied both, then `replaceState` dropped the override, so a refresh did not open the same board. Names were case-sensitive (`?Preset=Stones` missed) and `seats=Human, Hunter` missed on the space. A manual slider left the select on its placeholder but left `?preset=` in the bar, so a refresh restored the bundle and threw the tweak away. `Object.assign` of a preset also shared its `seats` array with the caller.
- **The competition file did not match the code.** `experiments/results/competition.json` still said random 274, keeper 241, hunter 246. Re-running the same seeds on that code already gave keeper 446 and hunter 439 against random 106 over 4 games — the file was a measurement from before the bot changes, copied forward. There was no `matchup.js`.
- **The rim feed did not capture, and the value function would not have taken a capture if it had.** From the launch lane the lock band is about 4° wide and 0.03 m/s wide (1.07–1.13 m/s), on the co-rotating tangent. The group's ±0.12 m/s jitter never landed in it. And a fly-by that is on red at t = 4 s reads the same terminal rate as a lock (~8 /s), so with a 40 s horizon the two priced within a few percent and the fly-by's larger window integral won. Empty-board first throws locked 0/6. The "bots can't play the hemisphere" result was that ranking, not a missing aim.

## Presets and links

- `parseQuery` folds preset, schema, recycle, theme and seat names, trims seats, and copies a preset's seat list before returning it.
- Boot applies the query and does not touch the address bar. Picking a preset from the panel writes `?preset=<name>` (and `ring=0` / `ghost=0` when those toggles are off). Any later control change writes the keys that differ from the shipped table, so the bar matches the panel.
- `experiments/client-smoke.js` covers the case-fold, the override that must survive a refresh, and the rewrite that drops a stale preset.

## Bots

- `stableRates` (`js/score.js`) is the locked-or-asleep red rate. `predictStroke` records it at the start, integrates score only over the handoff window, then follows the thrown puck for at most 1.5 s so a capture that lands just past handoff still counts. Recovery and losses stay the handoff figures — that is when the turn actually ends.
- `chooseStroke` samples a jitter-free feed grid (three radii, three speed scales, four angles) and one refine patch around the winner. The old jittered group 4 is skipped so the other groups keep their rng stream.
- The page shows a geometric aim during the 0.7 s think and searches once, at the throw. Searching at arm time and again at the throw was a second full decision on the UI thread for a stroke that was thrown away.

Empty-board first throws, after the change: 18/18 lock on rim6 (stable rate 3.3–8.0 depending on phase; one sixth of the period still lands in a gap) and 18/18 lock on classic at rate 8.0.

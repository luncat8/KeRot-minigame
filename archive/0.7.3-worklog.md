# 0.7.3 — return-zone and hole visual styles

A round of visual-only work on the return zones and the black holes. The fetched
commit `1b5935ce` is a divergent, simpler line by the same author (it renames the
styles to `straight`/`wavy`/`arc` and `flush`/`rounded`/`hemis`); its ideas were
adapted into this branch's richer zone/hole system rather than adopted, so the
rule geometry (`js/rules.js`, `js/physics.js`) is untouched.

## What changed

- **Dashed reach ring off the black.** The hole's dashed capture ring (the locus of
  a swallowed puck's centre, `r + puckR`) was stroked even when the *holes filled
  black* option was on, so it sat on the black. It now rides the ice only: it is
  skipped whenever a capture disk is filled (`js/render.js`, `drawRoundHole`).
- **Wavy marking is regular and is the default.** `wavyHatch` was rebuilt from two
  incommensurate sines (irregular ripples) to a single even sine — regular,
  parallel swells. `CFG.zoneStyle` defaults to `'wavy'` (was `'hatch'`), matched in
  the preset, the panel radio and the shipped-shape check.
- **Two new straight-wedge markings.** `'white'` (white lines on the ice) and
  `'snow'` (a snow field with dark lines), through a small `zonePaint` colour table
  keyed by `zoneStyle`; the field zones keep the teal pair.
- **`domes` hole shape.** A new `holeStyle` that paints each round void as a faded
  dome (a baked radial gradient, light at the top to dark at the rim) instead of a
  flat black disc.
- **Water holes.** A new view checkbox (`holeWater`, URL `holewater=1`) that fills
  each capture disk with a blue base and strokes a field of light-blue water waves
  inside it, instead of black. Water wins over black when both are on.
- **Zone/hole look swap.** A new view checkbox (`swap`, URL `swap=1`) that swaps the
  *look* of the return zone and the holes: the return zone is drawn as a void and
  the holes are drawn as zones. The launch lane (right, near the start) keeps the
  zone marking, so only the wedge swaps. Field zones, which have no separate wedge
  and lane, take the void look over their whole traced region. Purely cosmetic —
  the rules are unchanged.

Both view toggles follow the `holeFill` pattern: `opts.holeWater` / `opts.swap`,
echoed into the live link and preset echoes, parsed from the URL, carried in the
editor's copied setup, and absent from the minigame bundle (which renders as before).

## Validation

Passed:

- `node experiments/client-smoke.js` — the reach ring is gone from a filled disk and
  still rides the ice when unfilled; the water option fills and strokes; the swap
  paints the holes as zones (`fill:evenodd` each) and also renders a field zone as a
  void; `domes` paints a faded body; `white`/`snow` markings draw. No exceptions.
- `node experiments/zone-shapes.js` — every zone × hole × rule combination draws
  (now 120 frames), the default preset restores the shipped shapes (`wavy`/`classic`),
  URL keys and unknown-shape dropping unchanged.
- `node experiments/zone-edit.js`, `fx-smoke.js`, `minigame-smoke.js` — green; the
  editor's copied setup carries the two new view keys and round-trips them.
- `node tools/build-minigame.js --check` — the bundle matches its sources.
- `determinism.js`, `timing-check.js`, `speed-vote.js`, `settings-revive.js`,
  `field-preview.js`, `energy.js`, `eject-radius.js`, `overlap-centroid.js`,
  `rim-*.js`, `vote-rules.js`, `scoring.js`, `lock-band.js`, `mass-load.js`,
  `no-move.js` — green.

Note: `zone-catch.js` reports `FAIL both halves of wedge+lane catch something` on the
unmodified baseline too (the launch lane catches 0 % of deaths in its Monte-Carlo),
so it is a pre-existing failure, not a regression from this work.

## Next step

The water and swap looks are baked per geometry; a per-hole water density or a
subtle animation of the water swells could make the blue holes read more as water.
The multiplayer plan (`0.8-multiplayer-plan.md`) is ready for a first `js/net.js` +
relay prototype against the host-serialized protocol.

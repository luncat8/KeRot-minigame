# 0.6.9 worklog — Motion-ended turns and the Staller

Design: [`0.6.9-plan.md`](0.6.9-plan.md).

## 0. Review of 0.7.4

0.7.4 gave `feedTangent` a per-board speed factor `kv` (`feedKv`) and the bots use it.
The pre-throw bot aim marker in `game.js` (`showBotAim`) still scaled the feed speed by
the old constant 0.9, so on small disks the drawn aim disagreed with the stroke being
evaluated. It now uses `feed.v0 * feed.kv`, the same speed the bots throw.

## 1. Steps

1. **Rule** (`cfg.js`): `turnEnd`, `motionTurns`, `slideTime`, `turnLimit` (watchdog
   `8 + 3 · slideTime` in motion mode, `handoffT` otherwise), `turnSpan`, `futurePay`.
   `game.js` hands over at `isSettled || resolveT >= turnLimit`, and `setTurnEnd` is live
   in play.
2. **Predictor** (`predict.js`): `predictHorizon`, `pred.window` and `pred.settled`.
   `pred.stallLead` is the lead integrated with evolving rates past the window, plus
   `stallTail` (an extrapolated tail when something still slides at the horizon,
   negative time when the board settles early).
3. **Bots** (`bots.js`): every search horizon reads `predictHorizon`. `futurePay` replaces
   the inline later-turn formula in the bots, warp, score reference and diag scripts.
   - New `staller` profile with `stall: 1.5`. The term is gated on the sustained rate
     lead before the throw (see §3).
4. **Surfaces:**
   - `players.js` (seat type), `minigame/entry.js` (`turnEnd` option, bot names),
     `docs/snowfall-integration.md`;
   - `render.js` timer bar (a sweeping block in motion mode);
   - `ui.js` (radios, link key `turns`, mode-aware status line);
   - `presets.js` (`TURN_ENDS`, the `staller` preset);
   - `index.html` (the "a turn ends" fieldset, the "turn end" help section).
5. **Experiments:**
   - New `experiments/motion-turns.js` (rule, agreement and staller gates, plus
     `--bench N` and `--span`).
   - `time-warp.js` gains `KEROT_TURNS=motion`.
   - `client-smoke.js` covers the turn rule in the bar, the radios, the link, the preset
     and the default reset. Its ghost horizon assert now reads `predictHorizon`.
   - `bot-strategies.js` loop guard now uses `turnLimit`.

## 2. Measurements

Turn length, motion mode, 4-bot leagues (default board μk 0.03, watchdog 38.6 s):

| run | turns | mean | p50 | p90 | max | watchdog hits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| window, 8 games | 205 | 3.77 | 4.02 | 4.02 | 4.02 | 143 (= handoffT) |
| motion, 8 games | 206 | 5.47 | 5.00 | 8.42 | 13.25 | 0 |

`--bench 8` standings (`experiments/results/motion-turns.json`). In the window the staller
is its twin.

| bot | window score / wins | motion score / wins | motion turn mean | ms/decision (motion) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| staller | 352 / 1 | 378 / 2 | 5.78 | 594 |
| keeper | 350 / 2 | 302 / 1 | 5.28 | 570 |
| balanced | 335 / 1 | 359 / 3 | 5.42 | 556 |
| timing | 359 / 4 | 359 / 2 | 5.32 | 548 |

In motion mode a decision costs ~1.3× the window's, because the horizon is up to
`maxResolve` (8 s) instead of 4 s. Lowering `maxResolve` is the knob if a slow device
needs it: it bounds the search, not the turn.

`--span` checks the turn length across friction (2 games each) against
`0.9 · turnSpan = 0.9 · (2.06 + 1.54 ln slideTime)`:

| μk | slideTime | mean turn | model | max / watchdog |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0.008 | 38.2 | 6.74 | 6.90 | 12.9 / 122.7 |
| 0.012 | 25.5 | 6.27 | 6.34 | 15.3 / 84.5 |
| 0.02 | 15.3 | 5.86 | 5.63 | 15.2 / 53.9 |
| 0.03 | 10.2 | 5.44 | 5.07 | 12.2 / 38.6 |
| 0.05 | 6.1 | 4.26 | 4.36 | 13.8 / 26.3 |
| 0.08 | 3.8 | 3.65 | 3.71 | 8.1 / 19.5 |
| 0.15 | 2.0 | 2.41 | 2.84 | 7.0 / 14.1 |

The worst model error is 18 %, at the grippiest board. No turn came within half of its
watchdog.

The first `turnSpan` (linear in slideTime) overshot slick boards and undershot sticky
ones. The first watchdog (`3 · slideTime`) was 6.1 s at μk 0.15, where measured turns
reached 7 s. Both were refit from this sweep.

## 3. The staller gate

The first staller added `stall · stallLead` unconditionally. When behind, the lead is
negative, so the term priced every stroke by how quickly it ended the turn. Paired
leagues (6 games, motion mode) showed the cost:

| staller variant | staller score | others | turn when ahead | turn when behind |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| stall 0 (twin) | 573 | 461 | 6.21 | 5.53 |
| stall 1.5, ungated | 378 | 489 | 5.85 (n 1) | 5.27 |
| stall 1.5, gated | 566 | 448 | 9.35 (n 10) | 5.71 |
| stall 4, gated | 550 | 451 | 9.85 (n 8) | 5.57 |

With the gate (sustained rate lead before the throw > 0), the score is back at the
twin's level, and turns run ~50 % longer when ahead. When behind it plays its twin's
stroke, which `motion-turns.js` now asserts exactly. `stall: 1.5` keeps the best score
of the gated variants.

## 4. Validation

- `motion-turns.js`: all gates, including `--bench 8` (0 watchdog hits) and `--span`
  (worst 18 %).
- Window mode is bit-identical to the previous release: paired game scores against a
  worktree of the base commit match exactly.
- `determinism` (θ 1.5661999999999634 ×3), `client-smoke` (14 presets, new turn-rule
  checks), `timing-check`, `speed-vote`, `settings-revive`, `scoring`, `zone-edit`,
  `zone-shapes`, `fx-smoke`, `bot-strategies` (12 games) and `warp-wins` (4 games) pass.
- `minigame-smoke` passes, and `tools/build-minigame.js --check` matches its sources.
- `KEROT_TURNS=motion time-warp.js 6 3 0.5 4`: all gates pass, including copy-vs-game
  parity.
- Default `time-warp.js` fails only its two wall-clock gates, "a try is bounded work"
  (282 ms) and "frame slice" (80 ms). The base commit fails both identically on this
  2-core sandbox (287 ms, 80 ms), so they are environmental.

## Next step

- `turnSpan` is fit on the classic board with this four-bot league. A board whose
  strokes are mostly locks (a big disk, `scoreScale`) settles sooner. If the 0–999 scale
  drifts there, fit `MOTION_SPAN_S` per preset, or measure `turnSpan` online from the
  game's own finished turns.
- The staller only stretches turns it already leads. A natural follow-up is a counter
  for human seats: show the active seat's rate lead in the status line during a motion
  turn, so a player can see why the board is still running.

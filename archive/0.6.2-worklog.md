# 0.6.2 — free-floating scratches, worn hex decor, a stereo listener

Scope (prompt in `0.6.2-draft.md`, architecture in `0.6.2-plan.md`): the big disk's
radial decor lines get a random length and position; a settings choice swaps them for
a worn hexagonal etching; the sound layer gains a mic-head listener with stereo pan,
a facing setting and a volume slider defaulting to 1.3× the old fixed level.

## What changed

- `js/fx-glass.js`
  - `bakeDiskTex()` reads `decorMode`. `radial`: 300 scratches (was 190), each with a
    random angle, start radius (0.04–0.70 R) and length (0.05–0.43 R), capped at
    0.985 R — most touch neither the centre nor the rim. Shorter lines carry ~3× less
    ink than the old full-span brush, so the count went up and the light stroke got an
    alpha jitter (0.045–0.070); verified on a 2× crop of a bare patch.
  - `hex`: `bakeHexTex()` strokes a flat-top honeycomb (cell = R/5.2) at 192², drawn
    upscaled into the 512 sprite so no edge stays vector-sharp. Per cell, each edge is
    skipped with p = 0.32 — a shared edge lands bright (both cells kept it), faint
    (one) or missing (neither). No edge dedup needed; the double-draw is the wear.
  - A decor switch rebakes only the disk sprite (`bakedDecor`), never the floor or
    the pucks; re-applying the same decor is a no-op.
  - Listener: `panFor(x, y, cfg)` is the source direction's component on the
    right-ear axis. `face`: `launch` (mic on the disk centre facing the initial puck
    position), `up` (centre, facing −y), `bottom` (mic at the bottom edge below the
    centre, facing up). Every event sound routes through one StereoPannerNode shared
    by its partials; contacts pan by the puck or the pair midpoint, throws by the
    launch box, voids/recycles by the leaver's last tracked position (new `ppx/ppy`
    slots), the closing chime stays centred. The hiss got one panner on its chain,
    driven per frame by the speed-weighted mean pan of the sliding pucks — scalars
    only, no per-frame allocation.
  - `set(state)` now takes the FX state object; the master gain is `VOL × vol`,
    `vol` ∈ [0, 2], shipped default 1.3 (0.32 → 0.416 effective).
- `js/fx.js` — the controller state carries `decor`/`face`/`vol` with the shipped
  defaults; `set()` merges them, `apply()` hands the add-on the whole object, so a
  key set before the lazy load is already in force when the script lands.
- `js/presets.js` — `DECOR_MODES`, `FACE_MODES`, `vol` ∈ [0, 2]; `parseQuery` reads
  `decor`, `face`, `vol`; the `default` preset lists the shipped triplet.
- `js/ui.js` + `index.html` — three controls under the presentation row: **disk
  decor** (radial brush | worn hex cells), **listener** (on the disk facing the
  launch | facing up | below the disk facing up) and **sound volume** (0–200 %,
  130 % shipped). All three join `liveQuery()` when they differ from shipped.
- No renderer change, so `games/kerot.js` needs no rebuild — verified by
  `tools/build-minigame.js --check`.

## Validation

- `node experiments/fx-smoke.js` — **OK**. New gates: the hiss chain ends in a
  panner; volume 0.5/1.3 scale the master to exactly `VOL × vol`; a left-rail tap
  pans ≤ −0.9 facing up, a right-rail tap ≥ +0.9, facing the launch the top rail is
  on the left, and from the bottom edge the top rail is dead ahead (|pan| < 0.05);
  the decor switch rebakes +2 canvases for hex (sprite + soft layer), +1 for radial,
  and re-applying is free; the hex etching keeps 182 of 268 iterated cell edges —
  some missing, most kept. `parseQuery` round-trips the three new keys and clamps
  `vol=9` to 2.
- `node experiments/client-smoke.js` — **OK**. New gates: `?decor=hex&face=bottom&
  vol=0.8` pulls the three controls and the options, the readout shows 80 %, and a
  manual move back to a shipped value drops the key from the live link.
- `node experiments/minigame-smoke.js`, `tools/build-minigame.js --check`,
  `experiments/settings-revive.js`, `experiments/determinism.js` — **OK**, untouched
  paths green.
- `node experiments/render-frames.js --glass [--decor=hex]` — real PNGs of both
  finishes in both themes; the radial plate reads as brushed worn ice with no
  starburst, the hex cells are soft with visible gaps, and the score map and puck
  gauges stay legible in both. `GlassFX.set(true, false)` in this script moved to
  the object signature and it gained the `--decor=` flag.

## Next step

A natural follow-up is distance alongside direction: the pan says where a contact
happened around the listener, but a rim tap and a centre tap at the same bearing
sound identical. A mild lowpass and level falloff with distance from the mic head
(one `BiquadFilter` per event, or a pre-filtered second noise buffer) would make the
board read as a place, not a ring. Measure first whether the added node cost stays
inside the event rate limit before shipping it.

# 0.1.7 plan — the speed vote

An optional rule lets the seats set the disk speed during a game. Every seat holds a
vote; between two turns the votes are averaged and the disk runs to the result.

## The rule

`CFG.speedVote`, the panel fieldset "disk speed in play", URL key `vote`:

| value | the speed changes |
| --- | --- |
| `off` | only on the panel: the ω slider sets it at any time (admin) |
| `turn` | by a vote before every turn |
| `round` | by a vote before every round — when the turn order wraps to the first seat still holding a ball |

`CFG.voteMode`, URL key `votemode`: a vote is a **change** (`delta`, within
±`voteDeltaMax` = 0.5 rad/s) or an **exact** speed (`exact`, 0 – `omegaMax`).

Default: **`round` + `delta`** (the reasons are under "Default" below).

The `default` preset lists the rule. The `solo` preset turns it off: a vote of one
is the slider.

## The vote

- **One vote per seat, settable at any time:**
  - *don't care* (`VOTE_NONE`), the default — it abstains;
  - *keep* (`VOTE_KEEP`);
  - a *value* (`VOTE_SET`) — a change or an exact speed.

  A vote cast during the turns stands until the tally.
- **The vote phase.** The rule may call a vote when a turn ends: the 4 s turn timer
  runs out, or the board settles first. If it does, the game enters `VOTE` for
  `voteT` = 1 s.
  - The next seat is lit, but no puck is armed and a throw is refused.
  - The board keeps running. It does not score: a turn's window pays the seat whose
    throw made it, and the vote is nobody's turn.
  - The bots cast their votes as the phase opens.
  - The game's first turn has no turn timer before it, so no vote either.
- **The tally,** when the phase closes:
  - the speed is the mean of the votes cast;
  - *keep* counts as a change of 0 (`delta`) or as the current speed (`exact`);
  - with no vote cast, the speed stays;
  - the result is clamped to [0, `omegaMax`];
  - every vote then goes back to don't care.
- **Rule changes:**
  - a rule or mode change applies from the next handoff and drops the votes held,
    since a value means something else in the other mode;
  - switching the rule off during a vote closes it uncounted.

## The motor

- A tallied change is a ramp, not a jump. The disk runs to the new speed in
  `spinRampT` = 1 s at constant angular acceleration α. `theta` is the exact integral
  of the ramp.
- **A rider pays for the tangential acceleration too.** It is held while
  d · hypot(ω², α) ≤ μs_eff · g. Without a ramp (α = 0) this is the old test
  bit for bit. So a fast change sheds a rider that a slow one keeps.
- The end-of-game spin-down is the same kind of ramp, to 0 over `settleTime` = 3 s.
  As it starts, it sheds the riders beyond μs_eff·g / hypot(ω², ω/settleTime). At
  the defaults that is 0.662 m, against a steady hold radius of 0.720 m.
- A still disk that starts turning wakes the pucks asleep on it. A sliver over the
  rim that the floor holds stays asleep.
- **The configured speed.**
  - A game's votes move `CFG.omega`. Game remembers the speed they started from, and
    the next game starts from it again.
  - A direct speed change — slider, auto-ω, a preset, a URL — is a new configured
    speed. The disk jumps there and any ramp is dropped.
  - A shared link carries the configured speed, never a voted one.

## Bots

`chooseVote(profile, world, cfg, seat, roster, next, rng)` in `js/vote.js`. The bot
weighs every speed it could ask for — the current one ± `voteDeltaMax`, or the whole
range for an exact vote — by two things.

1. **The riders.** A rider survives the ramp if the motor test above holds it.
   - Each kept rider pays its red rate: × opportunity if it is the bot's own,
     × −denial if it is an enemy's.
   - Riders are particular pucks, so they count in full, like the knock-offs of
     `chooseStroke`.
2. **The room for the throws before the next vote.** This is `holdableRate`: the
   disk's red rate averaged over every spot a whole puck can sit on, with spots
   outside the hold radius counting as nothing. It is weighted by 0.5, since about
   every other throw parks.
   - The bot's own throw counts × opportunity.
   - An enemy's throw counts × denial / (seats − 1). Room is the same for every
     thrower, so a speed that gives everyone the same room moves no margin by
     itself. What is left is the profile's lean: a keeper wants a disk that holds,
     a black hole one that holds nothing.
   - Under `turn` only the next seat's throw counts. Under `round`, one throw per
     seat that still holds a ball.

How the vote is chosen:
- If the spread of values is under 2 % of the mean parked rate, the vote is
  *don't care*.
- Otherwise the bot picks the best speed. Among equal ones it takes the nearest
  (a change also shakes what it did not aim at), and the profile's noise goes on
  the pick only.
- It then takes the plateau of speeds within that 2 % around the pick. If the
  plateau includes the current speed, the vote is *keep*. Otherwise it asks for the
  plateau's middle, which averaging with the other votes is least likely to push
  off it.

## Panel, board, links

- **Panel fieldset:**
  - three rule radios and the two mode radios;
  - one vote row per human seat: don't care / keep / slider / value. The bots vote
    on their own;
  - a status line: the vote is open and closes in t s, or the last tally
    (from → to, votes cast).

  The vote block is hidden while the rule is off.
- **The ω slider** follows the live speed. Its readout adds "(voted; set x)" while a
  game's votes have moved it.
- **The state line** reads "speed vote — Pn throws when it closes".
- **The board HUD:**
  - every seat's vote shows at the right of its cell: "+.10", "1.20", "=", or blank
    for don't care;
  - during a vote, its time drains in a bar under the whole row;
  - labels are made once per change, so the frame loop does not concatenate strings.
- **Help drawer:** a "disk speed in play" section, plus the URL keys `vote`
  (off | turn | round) and `votemode` (delta | exact).

## Default

`round` + `delta`. `experiments/vote-rules.js` plays the same seeded bot tables
through the real game under every variant; the results are in
`experiments/results/vote-rules-*.json`. Over two tables on two disk patterns:

| rule | game length vs fixed speed | mean ω (set: 0.783) | riders shed per game by tallies |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `round` + `delta` | +4 to +7 s | 0.72 – 0.81 | 0.9 – 2.0 |
| `round` + `exact` | +7 to +36 s | 0.89 – 1.04 | 3.2 – 5.3 |
| `turn` + `delta` | +16 to +45 s | 0.90 – 1.05 | 2.8 – 8.6 |
| `turn` + `exact` | +29 to +117 s | 1.21 – 1.38 | 4.1 – 6.8 |

Reasons:

- Every seat in a round throws at the same speed. Under `turn` each throw gets its
  own speed, set by the other N − 1 seats, which want that thrower to have less room:
  a gang-up on the next thrower every turn.
- About four pauses of 1 s per game (3.9 – 4.8 measured: returned pucks add rounds),
  instead of one before every throw (17 – 64).
- A change vote bounds every tally to ±0.5 rad/s. With exact votes, one extreme vote
  moves the average by up to omegaMax / seats. A random or trolling seat can then
  slam the disk, and a big change sheds riders through its own ramp.

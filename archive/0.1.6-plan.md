# 0.1.6 plan — seats (N players × 4 balls) and red-area scoring

Reference prompt: `0.1.6-draft.md`. Supersedes the single-player pool rules of
0.1.1–0.1.5; everything else about the board (physics, stroking, ghost, return
zones, handoff timer) stays.

## 1. Data model

- `js/players.js` (new, node-safe): the roster. A seat is
  `{ type, balls, score }` — `type ∈ {human, random, keeper, hunter}`, `balls` starts
  at `CFG.ballsPerPlayer` (4), `score` accumulates raw `red·m²·s`. `SEAT_COLORS` lives
  here and is the one palette (render + panel read it).
  API: `makeSeat`, `createRoster`, `nextSeat(roster, from)` (first seat with a ball,
  circular, −1 when the round is over), `seatsHaveBalls`, `leader`.
- `js/physics.js`: the world gains `own: Uint8Array` — the seat index a puck belongs
  to; `spawnPuck(w, x, y, owner)` sets it (owner defaults to 0, so the physics
  experiments keep their 3-argument calls). `MAX_PUCKS` 32 → 40 (8 seats × 4 balls
  can all be on the board at once, plus headroom).
- `js/cfg.js`: `poolSize` → `ballsPerPlayer: 4`, `maxPlayers: 8`,
  `scoreDisplayMax: 999`, `maxThrows` 48 → 96 (a board that keeps handing pucks back
  still ends).

## 2. Scoring (`js/score.js`, new, node-safe)

- Density = the red channel of `fieldColor(fieldLocal(...))`, i.e. exactly the pixel
  the disk is painted with. Baked once into a grid over the disk's bounding square
  (`SC_LUT_N = 96` → 1.7 cm cells, `(n+1)²` nodes), read with a clamped bilinear.
- Area = the puck's own equal-area sample pattern (`FR_RINGS × FR_RAYS` cells,
  exported from physics.js): each cell is `πr²/N` of area and counts only if it lies
  on the disk. Cell offsets are world-axis; the disk test and the lookup both happen
  in disk-local coordinates (`Rot(−θ)`), so a puck riding the disk has a constant
  rate, which is the whole point.
- `scoreTick(w, roster, dt)`: one pass over the pucks, `roster[own].score += dt·rate`.
  Called once per frame from `Game.afterSteps` with the simulated `dtSim`, so it is
  frame-rate independent (dt sums to the same total at any display rate).
- `scoreRate(w, seat)`: the same quantity without the `dt` — used by the predictor so
  the bots optimise what the scoreboard counts.
- `scoreRef(cfg, seats)` / `displayScore(cfg, seats, raw)`: raw → 0…999.

## 3. Turn flow (`js/game.js`)

- `armTurn()`: `nextSeat` from the current seat, take one ball from it, spawn the
  puck on the launch spot with `own = turn`, prep the bot if the seat is one. Runs
  out of balls → `settle`.
- `afterSteps`: inside `resolve` (and `settle`, the board is still live) accumulate
  the scores; at `isSettled || resolveT ≥ handoffT` recycle, hand the turn to the next
  seat, re-arm or settle; a recycled puck goes back to **its owner's** stock
  (`giveBack(owner) → roster[owner].balls++`).
- Roster edits are live: `addPlayer(type)` pushes a seat (and restarts a finished
  game), `removePlayer(i)` refunds the puck on the launch spot, drops that seat's
  pucks from the board, splices the roster and re-arms if it was his turn.
- Space / pointer input only acts for a `human` seat on turn.

## 4. Presentation

- `js/render.js`: pucks are stroked in their owner's colour; a HUD along the top of
  the board draws one cell per seat (colour dot, balls left as dots, displayed score),
  highlights the seat on turn and shows the turn window draining (resolveT/handoffT).
- `js/ui.js` + `index.html`: a roster block — per seat a colour dot, a type select,
  balls, score and a × button, plus "+ add player"; the final breakdown lists the
  displayed scores and the winner.

## 5. Validation

- `experiments/scoring.js` (new): LUT vs exact `fieldColor` on the disk; the pattern's
  area vs the analytic circle–circle lens area over a sweep of overlaps; cost per
  puck-rate in ns; raw and displayed scores of a 2-seat bot game.
- `experiments/client-smoke.js`: the render/game smoke with seats — HUD rows, add and
  remove mid-game, a 2-seat bot game reaching `scored`, scores monotone and finite.
- `experiments/compete.js`: seats play each other; per-bot mean displayed score, plus
  throws/recycles/board, into `experiments/results/`.
- `experiments/zone-catch.js`: same gates, new seat/round API.

# 0.6.9 plan — Motion-ended turns and a Staller bot

Opt-in turn rule beside the fixed window, and a **Staller** character that plays it.
Separate from the fixed-window **Timing** bot (0.6.8), which keeps its strategy.

## Rule

`CFG.turnEnd`: `'window'` (default, unchanged) or `'motion'`. Motion-ended turns need
friction (`motionTurns(cfg)` = `turnEnd === 'motion' && muK > 0`); without it the fixed
window stays in force, because nothing would ever settle.

- **One handoff test everywhere:** `isSettled(w) || resolveT >= turnLimit(cfg)` in the
  game, the predictor, bot rollouts, warp copies and the embedded game.
- **Motion** means a puck in `ST_SLIDE`. A puck sleeps only when it is at rest *and* has
  stopped spinning (`epsSleep`, `epsSpin`), so residual spin keeps a turn open. Locked
  riders turn with the disk and sleepers are at rest; neither holds a turn. No extra quiet
  interval: the turn hands over on the first settled step, as the window's early exit
  already does.
- **Watchdog** (motion mode only): `turnLimit = 8 s + 3 · slideTime(cfg)`. `slideTime`
  is the longest a free puck can slide on bare ice: a full stroke, or a puck flung at
  the rim speed. Walls only take energy out. A rim straddler dragged by the motor adds a
  few seconds that do not shrink with friction, which is why the limit has a fixed base.
  In the window it is `handoffT`.
- `maxThrows` stays the game-length guard.

## Shared turn length

`turnSpan(cfg)` is the nominal turn: `handoffT` in the window, and `2.06 + 1.54 ·
ln(slideTime)` in motion mode. That is the least-squares fit of the measured mean turn
divided by 0.9, across μk 0.008–0.15. The score display's reference (`scoreRef`) and the
bots' value of later turns (`futurePay = 2 + 0.9 · turnSpan · turns`) both read it, so
the 0–999 scale and the bots' prices keep their meaning when turns run longer.

## Predictor

`predictHorizon(cfg)`: `handoffT` in the window. In motion mode it is
`min(turnLimit, maxResolve)`. This bound is a cost cap on the search, not a gameplay
cap: the real turn plays on past it.

- `pred.window` is the part of the turn that the score integral covers (≤ `handoffT`).
  The integral itself is unchanged, so the fixed window and the existing profiles price
  strokes exactly as before.
- `pred.stallLead` (motion mode only) is the thrower's rate lead over the strongest
  other seat, integrated over the turn time *past* that window.
  - It uses the rates as they evolve, so a lead that reverses mid-turn counts against
    the extra time.
  - A board that settles early contributes negative time (the rest of the window it
    gave up).
  - A board still sliding at the horizon extrapolates: the last lead is held for as long
    as its fastest slider needs to stop on bare ice, capped by the watchdog.
  - Because only time beyond the window enters, nothing is counted twice.

## Staller

`BOT_PROFILES.staller` is balanced-like, plus `stall: 1.5`. It adds `stall · stallLead`
to `evaluateStroke` only while its sustained rate before the throw (locked/asleep
pucks) beats every other seat's. When it is behind, it plays for position like its
twin. Banked score never enters the decision. In the window `stallLead` is 0, so the
fixed mode pays no duration reward.

## Surfaces

- **Settings:** the "a turn ends" radios (`window` / `motion`) are live in play.
  `Game.setTurnEnd` re-plans a waiting bot and redraws the ghost.
- **Link key:** `turns=motion`.
- **Preset:** `staller`, "motion turns · staller keeps it sliding", seats human,
  staller, keeper, timing.
- **Seat type:** `staller`.
- **Snowfall option:** `turnEnd`.
- **Timer bar:** a sweeping block in motion mode (no clock to drain); the countdown in
  the window.
- The status line and help text describe whichever rule is active.

## Validation

`experiments/motion-turns.js`:

- **Rule:** an unsettled throw outlives `handoffT` and hands over settled; a quiet board
  hands over at once; three locked riders do not hold the turn; the watchdog is finite
  and never shorter than the window; μk = 0 falls back to the window.
- **Agreement:** the predictor ends the turn within a frame of the game. A speed vote
  opens only on a settled board. The score reference and `futurePay` share `turnSpan`.
- **Staller, paired boards** against `stallerTwin` (the same profile with `stall: 0`):
  - ahead in positional income, it keeps the board moving longer;
  - behind, it plays its twin's stroke;
  - ±900 banked points never flip its pick;
  - in the window it is its twin.
- `--bench N`: seeded four-bot leagues in both modes, reporting turn-length distribution,
  watchdog margin, CPU per decision and standings. Writes `results/motion-turns.json`.
- `--span`: the turn-length fit against friction.

The fixed window stays bit-identical to the previous release. The determinism, scoring,
timing-check, speed-vote, settings-revive and client-smoke suites, `time-warp`
(`KEROT_TURNS=motion` too) and the minigame bundle check all pass.

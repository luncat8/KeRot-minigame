## Decisions

- Shapes are CFG rules, so the triangle swallows by its own shape, the rounded zone
  hugs whatever the holes are, and the arcs zone includes the holes. Absorption runs
  every step, so a puck touching a void is gone before any handoff recycles it — no
  extra exemption needed for "holes first".
- Triangle size: area of the semicircle, so a shape change is not a difficulty change.
- Inset lines on a 2.5 cm grid: first bake measured ≈ 120 ms in the harness, which turned
  out to be the direct-eval scope lookups; with `(0, eval)` it is 24 ms (rounded) and
  9 ms (arcs) for both modes, the coarse line grid included.
- Score card corners are page state (`cards`), not a rule.

# 0.6.7 plan — 3-rounded outlined by the three circles alone

`3-rounded` is the wedge cut by three bigger circles — the disk and each hole
grown by `puckR + ZONE_CUT_GAP` — with a strip of ice between rim and outline.
The gap is 0.06 m. The shapes are rules, not skins: whatever is drawn is
exactly the region the rule tests.

## Return zone

- **3-rounded, wedge side (`advance`).** The wedge sides run hole → disk
  centre, not corner → disk centre. Each side segment lies inside the union of
  the disk and hole circles: the circles overlap along it (centre distance
  1.263 m, radii 0.96 m and 0.34 m), so no point of either side is in the
  zone and no straight edge reaches the outline. The outline is the three
  circles' arcs alone — the disk arc on the right, one hole arc at each wall —
  meeting at the circles' crossings. The hole cuts are circles around the hole
  centres whatever the void's own shape, so a triangle void still gets a
  circular cut (the circle covers its reach). The launch lane joins as before.
- **3-rounded, fan side (`zones`).** The holes are far from the fan, so the
  fan's own sides stay; the disk cut and the (far) hole cuts are as above.
- **6-hemi** is unchanged except the shared gap: the half-board through the
  disk centre cut by one bigger circle, now grown by the 6 cm gap.
- Rendering is unchanged: marching squares over the same `zoneField`
  (outline 1 cm, inset lines 2.5 cm, levels every 8 cm). The inset lines are
  the field's levels — arcs of the three circles, meeting at their crossings.

## Validation

`node experiments/zone-shapes.js`: drawn field region = rule region on
40 000 random points per shape and mode; 3-rounded keeps the gap clear of the
disk and the hole circles and stays inside the wedge; both hole → centre side
segments are covered by the circles (no exposed point); a point on the old
corner → centre side is zone interior; with triangle voids no zone point lies
inside a void's reach; the lane stays in; all 72 shape × rule combinations
draw. Renders: `node experiments/render-frames.js --mode=advance
--zone=3-rounded` (and `--mode=zones`, `--zone=6-hemi`).

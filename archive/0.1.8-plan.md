# 0.1.8 plan — review speed-vote strategy

## Review question

A speed-up can deny the next throw room, but it also changes whether existing pucks
stay on the disk. Do not describe it as a generally good bot move: the choice must
include the score-bearing riders that the ramp could shed.

## Decision

- Keep the `round` + `delta` default. A `turn` vote is explicitly targeted at one next
  throw and can create a majority incentive to deny that seat; `round` compares one
  own throw with the mean enemy throw instead.
- Keep speed choice as a tradeoff, not a hard rule that forbids speed-ups. Candidate
  value includes locked own riders by their red score rate and checks their survival
  against the ramp's centripetal and tangential acceleration. A bot may still accept
  a sacrifice if its denial-weighted gain is larger.
- Regression-test both sides of that trade: a safe high-scoring own rider can coexist
  with a useful speed-up that sheds an enemy rider; a threatened high-scoring own
  rider should stop a bot from voting up when that is the only board consequence.

## Follow-up fixes

- Price a requested ramp from the motor speed expected when the vote closes if an
  earlier ramp is still running; do not treat its target as its live speed. Also do
  not count a currently locked rider that the older ramp will shed before closure.
- Build exact-vote candidates with both range endpoints and the current setpoint,
  even when the candidate spacing does not divide the range. Find the plateau and its
  midpoint in speed units; keep delta-vote requests on their displayed 0.01 rad/s grid.
- Make the panel's omega slider and readout follow live motor speed during a ramp,
  while showing the voted target and configured starting speed separately.
- A direct slider change updates the live speed immediately; if it starts a stopped
  disk, wake pucks the disk can no longer leave asleep.

## Gates

- `node experiments/speed-vote.js`: ramp-start forecast, threatened-own-rider
  decision, useful safe-rider attack, exact candidate endpoints/current setpoint,
  direct wake on spin-up, vote tally and schedule.
- `node experiments/client-smoke.js`: live speed slider/readout during a ramp and the
  remaining panel/URL behavior.
- `node experiments/settings-revive.js`: direct omega changes and round restart.
- Re-run the physics and bot gates; compare vote-rule behavior against the 0.1.7
  measurements before retuning any profile weights.

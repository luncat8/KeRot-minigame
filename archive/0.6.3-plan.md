# 0.6.3 plan — ice as a material: light is spent, not added

Scope: the optional glass skin (`js/fx-glass.js`) only. No gameplay, no renderer hook,
no new asset. The plain renderer is the reference for how dark the board may be.

## The idea

A frozen rink seen from above is a *dark mirror*. The room above it is not lit — the
ceiling is a black field with a few thin tubes in it — and the ice spends light:
frost scatters it away, the paint below it is dimmer than the paint on the surface, and
a polished plate only returns a specular ghost of what hangs over it. Every layer the
skin adds should therefore either lower the local average or be a hard, thin highlight.
A texture is allowed to add light only if that light is a *speck* (smaller than a
texel of the eye's low-pass filter) or a *tube* (narrower than a centimetre per metre
of board).

The rule the whole round follows:

> the room is only ever as dark as the skin's *darkest* layer, and a lamp is only ever
> as interesting as its *contrast*.

## Layers

| layer | the light it moves | behaviour |
| --- | --- | --- |
| floor grain (`floorTex`) | ⊢ small white speckle, soft swaths, cool sky, vignette | zero-mean grain, weaker sky (0.055 instead of 0.14), deeper vignette (0.46) |
| veil (`veilTex`) | ⊣ broad clouds | cold depth tint (8,22,40) instead of white milk, so the veil *deepens* the whole board |
| glaze (`glazeTex`) | ⊢ milk | thinner, 0.018 → 0.15 across the plate, drawn at 0.42/0.46 instead of 0.54/0.58 |
| polish (`diskTex`) | ⊢ sheen, glow, streak | halved: the glow is 0.11, not 0.22, so the plate keeps its rim gradient |
| lamp shade | ⊣ multiply | the matte ceiling and the housing: a soft seat plus two shelves |
| lamp light | ⊢ lighter | a pool, a short smear, and the tube (bloom, body, filament, lips, fringe) |
| lamp cuts | ⊣ erase | ribs across the diffuser and free striae, punched out of the sprite |

## The lamp

A tube is the only part of the fixture that is actually bright, so the sprite is built
around the tube and every other mark is either very wide and very faint or very thin
and carved out of it:

- **position** board metres, five lamps in the two shimmer groups, never rotating with
  the disk;
- **seat** an ellipse (radius 0.72 × 0.46 of the length) at 0.34 black, plus two soft
  dark shelves either side of the tube; both drawn with `multiply`, since a reflection
  that only ever adds cannot darken the ice around the light;
- **tube** 12–19 mm wide and 0.28–0.50 m long. Four strokes with the same end-fade
  gradient: bloom (`w × 3.4`, a = 0.10), body (`w × 1.5`, a = 0.44), filament
  (`w × 0.46`, a = 0.95, near-white), and two lips (`w × 0.22`, a = 0.22) that give the
  diffuser edges;
- **colour** a warm ink (`255,242,214`), a whiter core and one cool fringe line
  (`150,198,238`) below the filament — one pixel of chromatic separation is what stops
  the bar reading as a white sticker;
- **mirror detail** a second, dimmer bar 2.6 tube-widths lower (the far face of the ice
  mirrors the tube again), seven cut glints along the filament, ribs across the
  diffuser at 2–4 irregular positions and six striae down the smear, all punched with
  `destination-out` so the light is *broken*, not painted over;
- **resolution** 200 px/m, four textures of 720 × 440 (light and shade per group),
  baked once. The old bake was 432 px wide, which could not carry a 1 cm bar;
- **cost** five `drawImage` calls per frame instead of three (the veil, then a multiply
  and an add per group), all from cached sprites. Worth it: a blend mode cannot be
  folded into the other pass.

Breathe: only the light pass pulses (±10 % on two slow, out-of-phase sines). The
housing is matte and does not breathe; `prefers-reduced-motion` freezes both.

## Budgets

The tone check (`experiments/render-frames.js --tone`) draws one fixed board plain and
with the skin and compares real pixels. Rec. 709 luma, patches in board metres:

| gate | patch | budget |
| --- | --- | --- |
| the ice does not lift the floor light | left edge, 0.15 × 0.40 | +5 |
| the ice does not lift the polished plate | on the disk, clear of lamps | +12 |
| the ice does not lift the whole frame | whole board | +5 |

"Light added" (the mean of every pixel the skin made brighter, after a 16 px block
average) and the share of it that falls inside a lamp footprint are printed as
information: the pucks and the plate are legitimately brightened by the skin, so a
share near 1 is not a reachable target and must not be a gate.

# 0.6.7 worklog — 3-rounded outlined by the three circles alone

## Review of the previous round

0.6.6 shipped `3-rounded` as the wedge (corner → disk centre sides) cut by
three bigger circles, with the wedge sides staying straight by decision. That
decision is what this round reverses: the holes lie outside the wedge, so
their circles only nick it, and where a side meets a hole circle the outline
spikes into a point and the inset lines follow it — the "curved strange
outlines" and the parent-shape leftovers. The 8 cm gap also read generous.

## Reading the request

The outline must be just the circular shapes — 2 from the holes, 1 from the
big disk — with no straight edge, and the clear-ice gap smaller, about 6 cm
instead of 8 cm.

## Steps

1. Rendered the shipped shape (`render-frames.js --mode=advance
   --zone=3-rounded`) and confirmed the spike at each hole: wedge diagonal +
   hole arc meeting at a point, inset lines following it.
2. `js/rules.js`: `ZONE_CUT_GAP` 0.08 → 0.06 m. The `3-rounded` wedge sides
   now run hole → disk centre (the same lines `rounded` already used as its
   closing sides), and the hole cuts are circles around the hole centres
   (`dist − r − puckR − gap`) whatever `holeStyle` is, instead of `holeGap`
   (which for triangle voids is a triangle). The fan side keeps its own sides.
3. `js/cfg.js`, `README.md`: the `3-rounded` description now says the outline
   is the three circles alone.
4. `experiments/zone-shapes.js`: the leak check uses the hole circles
   directly; new checks — both hole → centre segments covered by the circles,
   the old corner side is zone interior, triangle voids keep their reach
   clear under the circular cut.
5. Rebuilt `games/kerot.js`.

## Decisions

- **Sides through the holes, not the corners.** A hole → centre segment is
  covered by the disk and hole circles because they overlap along it: centre
  distance 1.263 m against radii 0.96 + 0.34 = 1.30 m with the 6 cm gap (the
  overlap is 3.7 cm, so the segment's field peaks at −0.019 m — covered, with
  margin). No straight edge can reach the outline, and the zone is the left
  strip bounded by the disk arc, the two hole arcs and the walls. The old
  corner sides are gone entirely: a point on one, outside both circles, is
  now zone interior.
- **Circular hole cuts for every void shape.** `3-rounded` is defined by
  circles, so the cut is a circle around the hole centre even for triangle
  voids. With the 6 cm gap the circle (radius 0.34 m) still covers the
  triangle's reach (farthest point 0.225 m + puckR 0.10 m = 0.325 m), so no
  zone point lies inside a void's reach — checked for both void styles.
- **Gap 0.06 m, shared with 6-hemi.** The cut pair's one constant, so
  `6-hemi`'s disk circle shrinks with it. The inset-line step stays 8 cm —
  it is decoration inside the zone, not the clear-ice gap. 6 cm is also near
  the floor for this geometry: below it the disk and hole circles stop
  overlapping along the side and a straight stub would reappear.
- **Fan side unchanged in shape.** The holes are on the far side of the board
  from the fan, so no circle can cover the fan's sides; they stay straight,
  as in 0.6.6. Only the wedge mode was asked to be circular.

  their crossings, no straight segment; the gap reads a little tighter.

## Next step

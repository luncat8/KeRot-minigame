# 0.6.3 — ice spends light, and the lamps become tubes

Scope (prompt in `0.6.3-draft.md`, design in `0.6.3-plan.md`): the glass skin was
lighter and hazier than the plain renderer, and its five ceiling reflections were wide
bright bars that read as white smudges. The round turns the lighting around — every
layer either deepens the ice or is a thin, hard highlight — and rebuilds a reflection
out of a fixture instead of a glow.

## What changed

- `js/fx-glass.js`
  - **Floor** (`bakeFloorTex`): the light grain drops from 34/13/5 to 12/5/2 alpha, the
    soft frost swaths from 0.028 to 0.016, the fine scratches from 0.14 to 0.085, the
    cool sky from a white 0.14 to a blue-grey 0.055 and the vignette deepens from 0.38
    to 0.46. The grain stays zero-mean, so the ice still reads as ice.
  - **Veil** (`bakeMilkTex` → `bakeVeilTex`, `milkTex` → `veilTex`): the broad clouds
    are now a cold depth tint (8,22,40) instead of white milk, with eleven faint frost
    blooms and the same 900 pit marks as the only light the veil adds. Drawn at
    0.20/0.26 alpha instead of 0.30/0.38. Paint under ice now *costs* light.
  - **Glaze and polish**: the milky radial on the plate is roughly halved (0.04/0.09/
    0.24 → 0.018/0.045/0.15), the frost patches go 0.05+0.08 → 0.03+0.05, the glaze is
    drawn at 0.42/0.46 (was 0.54/0.58), and `bakeDiskTex`'s sheen, glow and streak drop
    from 0.14/0.22/0.075 to 0.10/0.11/0.05. The plate keeps its rim gradient instead of
    turning into a pale pancake.
  - **Lamps**: `bakeLampTex(group, cfg, light)` now bakes two 720 × 440 textures per
    group (200 px/m, was 432 px across the board): a *shade* of black ink and a *light*
    sprite. `drawLampShade` lays a soft seat (0.34 black, ellipse 0.72 × 0.46 of the
    length) and two shelves along the tube. `drawLamp` builds the tube from a faint
    pool, a short vertical smear, a second dimmer bar (the far face of the ice), then
    bloom, body, filament and two diffuser lips with one cool fringe below — a warm ink
    `255,242,214`, a whiter core, a blue-grey fringe — plus seven cut glints, 2–4
    irregular ribs and six striae punched with `destination-out`, so the mirror is
    broken rather than over-painted. Tube widths are 12–19 mm against lengths of
    0.28–0.50 m (was 5.0–7.2 cm wide).
  - **Surface pass**: veil → per group `multiply` shade at 0.26/0.30 → `lighter` tube at
    0.32/0.36 × the breathing term. Only the light pulses; the housing is matte and
    stays put, and reduced motion still freezes the tube at mid brightness.
  - `GlassFX.lamps` exposes the position table for the tone check; nothing in the
    renderer reads it.
- Renderer, page, presets, physics: untouched — the skin's hook contract did not move
  (`surface` still draws once per frame, in board space), and `games/kerot.js` is
  unchanged (`tools/build-minigame.js --check` confirms).
- `experiments/render-frames.js`: the frame loop now prints a mean luma per PNG, and a
  new `--tone` mode renders one fixed board plain and with the skin on the same canvas
  and compares real pixels — three budgets (floor, plate, frame) plus a reported
  "light added / share inside a lamp footprint". It exits 1 on a miss, so it works as a
  gate, and it needs the same `npm install` in `experiments/tools` as the PNG mode.
- `experiments/fx-smoke.js`: the stub context now honours `save`/`restore` (without it
  a composite mode set inside `surface` leaked into the puck gates), `drawImage`
  records the blend mode, so the surface gate asserts the exact layer order
  `drawImage, shade, glow, shade, glow`, and a new gate checks that every mirrored lamp
  is a narrow tube (≤ 2 cm wide, at least 10× longer than wide).

## Validation

- `node experiments/render-frames.js --tone` — **ok** in both schemes:

  | gate | classic | kerot | budget |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | the ice does not lift the floor light | −13.9 | +3.3 | +5 |
  | the ice does not lift the polished plate | +10.1 | +8.6 | +12 |
  | the ice does not lift the whole frame | −4.9 | +3.2 | +5 |

  The same three gates on the previous design read +5.3 / +35.3 / +16.9 (classic) and
  +18.8 / +29.8 / +21.4 (kerot) — every one of them was a miss. The plate is the number
  the complaint was about: the disk used to come back 30 luma brighter than the plain
  one, now it is 9–10, which is the gloss the plate is allowed to keep.
- `node experiments/render-frames.js experiments/logs/frames-d --glass` and the same
  with `--decor=hex`, plus `--glass` plain renders — real PNGs at 1798 × 1100. Whole
  frame mean luma: kerot **72.8 plain → 76.0 glass** (was 94.2), classic **116.9 plain**
  **→ 112.1 glass** (was 133.9). On the disk: the centre reads 165.0 against the plain
  162.6 (was 183.1), the upper rim 125.6 against 107.9 (was 164.1), the floor at the
  left edge 24.8 against 21.2 (was 41.2), the return zone 63.7 against 58.8 (was 87.4).
  Looking at 4× crops of the tube, the fixture separates into a hot filament, a cooler
  body, two diffuser lips, a blue fringe under it, rib breaks and the striae; at 1:1 it
  is a 4–9 px line. Both finishes (radial, hex) and both schemes were inspected.
- `node experiments/fx-smoke.js` — **OK**. The new gates: the surface hook draws
  exactly `drawImage, shade, glow, shade, glow` per scheme, and all five lamps are
  tubes (widths 0.019/0.015/0.017/0.014/0.012 m). The baked-canvas count still holds
  (the sprite pair per group is baked once, and a theme or decor switch does not
  re-bake the lamps).
- `node experiments/client-smoke.js`, `node experiments/minigame-smoke.js`,
  `node tools/build-minigame.js --check`, `node experiments/settings-revive.js`,
  `node experiments/determinism.js` — **OK**, untouched paths green.

## Next step

The queued 0.6.2 follow-up still stands and is now the only place where the listener is
flat: distance. Contacts pan by direction, but a rim tap and a centre tap at the same
bearing sound identical. A mild lowpass plus a level falloff with distance from the mic
head (one `BiquadFilter` per event, or a pre-filtered second noise buffer) would make
the board read as a place rather than a ring — measured first against the event rate
limit.

On the visual side, the reflection is still a straight bar of constant width. A slight
taper toward the viewer and a sub-pixel parallax shift when the puck passes between the
eye and the lamp would sell the room; both are cheap, and the `--tone` gate now guards
the light budget they must stay inside.

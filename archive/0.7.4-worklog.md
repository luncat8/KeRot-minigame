# 0.7.4 worklog — Geometry-aware feed capture band (bots on `notsobigdisk`)

## 1. Diagnosis: why bots played bad on `notsobigdisk`

Reproduced with `experiments/repro-notsobig.js` and `experiments/diag-solo-compare.js`:
on the default board every bot but `random` saturates at ~323 raw solo / 105–446 in the
4-bot competition, while on the preset they scored 0–102 solo / 7–81 in competition.

The new `experiments/diag-lockband.js` sweeps feed radius × speed factor × angle and
prints where a throw actually locks, on both boards:

- **default**: locks at kv ∈ [0.88, 0.93] of the floor-friction solve `v0`, with
  **30–36 %** of the launch chord crossing the disk. The shipped grid
  `FEED_KV = [0.86, 0.90, 0.94]` sits right on that band — it was measured here.
- **notsobigdisk**: **no lock anywhere** in kv 0.82–1.14 with the shipped grid; the
  useful band (pucks that end on the disk and score) sits at kv **0.94–1.05**, while
  only **9.5 %** of the chord crosses the disk (puck radius is 40 % of the disk
  radius, so the three `FEED_FRAC` radii all clamp to the same cap). The shipped grid
  launched too slow: pucks fell short, slept in the bot's own box, scored nothing.

Root cause — the hardcoded sizes from the task brief: `feedTangent.v0` solves floor
friction over the **whole** chord, but the stretch that runs over the disk rubs a
co-moving surface and loses far less speed. How much less depends on how much of the
chord crosses the disk, so one fixed multiplier grid fits only the board it was
measured on. The band centre is ~0.90 on the reference board (33 % over disk) and
~1.00 on the small disk (10 % over disk).

## 2. Fix (`js/bots.js`, bundle rebuilt)

- **`feedTangent` now returns `kv`** — the band centre for the actual geometry:
  `sqrt((vs² + 2·μk·g·(L_floor + κ·L_disk)) / v0²)`, where `L_disk` is the chord
  stretch inside first contact (`diskR + puckR`, solved from the near quadratic root;
  distance to the centre falls monotonically to the tangent point) and
  `ON_DISK_KAPPA = 0.3202` is the fraction of floor friction a puck keeps while its
  body rides the moving disk. κ was fitted once so the reference board's middle feed
  radius lands at exactly 0.9000 — there the grid reproduces the old
  `{0.86, 0.90, 0.94}` bit-for-bit; on `notsobigdisk` it becomes
  `{0.928, 0.968, 1.008}`, straddling the measured 0.94–1.05 band.
- **`FEED_KV` → `FEED_DKV = [-0.04, 0, +0.04]`**, offsets around `f.kv`. Candidate
  counts are unchanged (3 speeds × 3 radii × 4 angles), so the rng draw stream stays
  aligned with the old grid and light (warp) decisions keep the same budget.
- Diagnostics updated to the new semantics: `diag-feedband.js`, `diag-landscape.js`;
  `diag-game6.js` now takes a preset argument. New measurement scripts:
  `diag-lockband.js` (band map), `fit-kappa.js` (κ fit + shipped-value cross-check),
  `probe-recycle.js` (where returned pucks sleep).
- `games/kerot.js` rebuilt (`node tools/build-minigame.js`, `--check` passes).

Small-disk games now also return overshoots from the `advance` wedge
(`recycled 8 → 19/game`): baseline pucks died short in the bot's own box and were
never seen again; with the corrected band they either park on the disk or overshoot
into the return wedge and get re-thrown — the dynamic the default board has always
played (~9 recycles/game).

Smokes: `node --check` on every changed file, `determinism.js`, `client-smoke.js`

## Next step

- κ is a one-parameter fit against a single reference board with the chord split
  taken at first contact. If intermediate disk sizes (board editor `disk size`
  handle, `scoreScale`) ever show a band offset, refit the split at half-overlap
  (`diskR`) or sweep κ per `patternScale` — `fit-kappa.js` prints the grid for any
  preset in one run.
- The spread ±0.04 is narrower than the measured small-disk band (±0.055). If a
  future board's phase jitter exceeds it, widen `FEED_DKV` — but keep its length at
  3 unless the rng-stream alignment is re-checked (`warp` light mode steps the
  radius and angle loops, not the speed loop).

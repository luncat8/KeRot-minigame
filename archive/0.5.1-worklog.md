# 0.5.1 worklog — shift precision ×8, and the look-ahead in wins

Two asks in one round: make shift+drag finer on angle and speed alike, and take up
the 0.5 worklog's next step — measure the time-warp look-ahead in wins, not in its
own ball annuities.

## Review of the previous pass

`archive/0.5-worklog.md` shipped the look-ahead with evidence in the planner's own
units (a search beats the greedy line by p50 0.62 of a ball *as the planner prices
it*) and flagged exactly why that number is an upper bound: rollouts answer with
`chooseStrokeQuick` while the real game plays searching bots. The suggested cut was
`compete.js` / `bot-strategies.js` with warp on vs off. Those two harnesses bypass
`createGame` — they call `chooseStroke` directly in a hand-rolled loop — while warp
lives in the game flow (`startPlan`/`stepPlan`, the held board), so a flag there
would have measured a different game. The measurement below is a new harness,
`experiments/warp-wins.js`, that plays the real game with nobody, one profile, or
everybody searching, same seeds throughout.

## Shift precision

- `CFG.preciseGain` 0.25 → 0.03125: shift is now 1/32 of plain, 8× finer than the
  old shift, on one shared gain for drag and arrows, angle and speed.
- `js/game.js` anchors the aim as well as the speed (`anchorRaw`/`anchorAng` beside
  `anchorLen`/`anchorSp`, `rebase` invalidating the angle anchor). A shift segment
  counts a `preciseGain` share of further drag on either axis; plain drag stays
  absolute and the press/release never jumps either way. The angle anchor starts at
  the drag's own direction — scaling an absolute angle would turn 180° into 6°.
- `nudge` uses the same gain instead of its own 5× steps (1° / 2 %+0.01 plain).
  Measured: 0.09 m of drag → 0.30 m/s plain, 0.0094 with shift; 0.09 m sideways →
  11.31° plain, 0.35° with shift; arrows 1° / 0.031° and 0.034 / 0.0011 m/s.
- `index.html` help now says fine aim and speed; `experiments/client-smoke.js`
  gates the speed slope, the aim slope, both no-jump transitions, and both nudge
  steps against `preciseGain`.

## Warp in wins

`experiments/warp-wins.js [games] [seed0] [schema] [table]` plays through
`createGame` (fixed speed, no bot delay): a baseline nobody-searching variant, one
variant per profile in which only that profile searches (the flag is toggled per
turn by direct `cfg` assignment — `setWarp` would cancel the plan), and an
everybody-searching variant. Seats rotate; game counts divisible by the seat count
keep the rotation even. Same `SEED0 + game·7919` rng in every variant, so a
searching profile faces the identical dice its greedy self faced.

Searching does not win more. Δ is the searching profile vs its own baseline:

Single-game σ ≈ 100 (3-seat) makes most cells noise individually, but 15 of 18
cells show zero or negative wins and no cell shows a large positive — the planner's
+0.62-ball self-report does not survive real replies. The one big score gain
(random +163) still wins nothing. Cost: ~3.9 s/game baseline, ~4.2 s asymmetric,
~4.4 s all-searching on 3 seats; ~10–12 s on 6 seats.

Two mechanisms, both in the accept rule and the rollout policy rather than the
search itself:

- The bar is gain over the *snapshot*, and the first candidate is the greedy line —
  so whenever greedy looks good the search stops after one try and never compares
  anything. On the crowded 6-seat boards balanced's greedy gains read +1.1…+2.7
  balls; all 27 of its searches took line 1 (7 one-try early stops, 2 best-of-six
  still line 1), hence byte-identical games. The search only shops when greedy
  looks bad.
- When it does shop (sparse 3-seat boards, multi-try searches), the lines it takes
  were priced against quick-policy replies and do not beat greedy against real
  ones — the upper-bound concern from 0.5, now measured.

Results kept in `experiments/results/warp-wins-*.json` (5 files).

## Validation

- `node --check` on every `js/` and `experiments/` file.
- `node experiments/client-smoke.js` — precision slopes, no-jump transitions,
  nudge steps, and the unchanged rest (render, votes, presets, warped smoke game).
- `node experiments/time-warp.js` — all gates pass; nothing in the planner moved.
- `warp-wins.js` determinism: same seeds give the same live-rng stream in every
  variant (one `chooseStroke` of rng per throw either way — rollouts use the copy's
  rng), so Δ is the search, not the dice.

## Next step suggestion

Fix the rollout policy or the accept rule, not the search — and re-measure with
`warp-wins.js`. The cheapest change to the rule is to compare lines against each
other instead of stopping at the first line that beats the snapshot (e.g. always
play out the ranked shortlist and take the best, keeping `warpGoal` as the bar for
the *ladder* only). The deeper fix is rollout replies that play closer to the
searching policy. Either way the gate is Δwins > 0 on classic ×24, not the
planner's own ruler — and keep an eye on 6-seat early-stop rates, which say whether
the search is comparing at all.

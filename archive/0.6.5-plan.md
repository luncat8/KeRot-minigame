# 0.6.5 plan — return zone and hole shapes

Two rule radio groups — the return zone's shape and the black holes' shape — plus a
page option for rounded score cards. The shapes are rules, not skins: whatever is
drawn is exactly the region the rule tests, as the plain wedge already is.

## Settings

| key | CFG / page | values | URL |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| return zone shape | `CFG.zoneStyle` | `hatch` (shipped), `wavy`, `rounded`, `arcs` | `zones=` |
| black holes | `CFG.holeStyle` | `classic` (shipped), `rim`, `well`, `triangle` | `holes=` |
| score cards | page `opts.cards` | `square` (shipped), `round` | `cards=round` |

Both rule keys are live (no reset): the renderer rebakes its zone/hole paths when it sees
a new value, and the ghost is re-simulated. The `default` preset lists all three.
The Snowfall minigame keeps the shipped shapes (it plays without return zones).

## Return zones

- **hatch** — the shipped wedge (or fan) minus the guard, 45° stripes, dashed outline.
- **wavy** — the same region and outline; each stripe wavers on two incommensurate sines,
  phases stepped by the golden angle.
- **rounded** and **arcs** are *soft* zones: the region `zoneField > 0` of one clearance
  field in `js/rules.js`, built with a polynomial smooth minimum (k = 0.3 m):
  - *rounded*, wedge side: `smin(guard, hole reach − puckR …, side(hole → disk centre) …)`.
    The hole terms make the outline hug each void; k is more than twice the 8 cm
    hole ↔ guard gap, so the outline bridges it with a fillet, burying the side lines,
    which only close the zone. No straight edge remains. Fan side: the fan's sides with
    the guard corners rounded. The lines are the field's levels every 8 cm — inset
    copies of the outline, rounded where it is.
  - *arcs*: `smin(guard, vertical through the disk centre)` — one curve from wall to wall
    around the disk; the holes lie inside the zone. Lines: circles around the disk
    centre every 8 cm, clipped to the zone.
  - The launch lane joins either (`max` with the strip's field) in `advance`.
- Rendering: marching squares over the same `zoneField`, chained into polylines so
  dashes run on along a contour. Outline: 1 cm grid, the field clamped just outside
  the walls so every contour closes into a loop (fill evenodd, the wall pieces fall
  outside the board clip). Inset lines: 2.5 cm grid. A rebake (style change or board
  geometry change in the fit view) costs ≈ 25 ms for both modes.
- Holes inside a zone need no extra rule: `absorbBlackHoles` removes a puck in the step
  it touches a void, long before a handoff could recycle it.

## Holes

- `holeGap(cfg, hole, x, y)` (physics) is the clearance to a void's shape; a puck is
  swallowed at `holeGap ≤ r`. Absorption, swept entry, the bots' black-hole aim and the
  rounded zone all go through it.
- **classic** — the shipped round void: dashed reach ring, violet rim, faint inner ring.
- **rim** — the round void with its rim only; the reach is implied.
- **well** — dashed reach, rim, a lit inner step at 0.58 r and a black core at 0.3 r.
- **triangle** — an isosceles triangle on its wall, apex inward, sides parallel to the
  wedge side (corner → disk centre: 45° on the shipped board), area equal to the
  semicircle's (h = r·√(π·tanα/2)), so it eats as much ice. A nested copy at 0.6 scale on
  the same wall point. Swept entry: the triangle grown by the puck radius is convex, so
  the first contact is an edge face pushed out by r or a corner circle of radius r.

## Score cards

`cards=round`: the seat cells get 0.05 m corners; the lit seat's border is inset by half
its width so the corners stay whole.

## Validation

`node experiments/zone-shapes.js`: drawn soft region = rule region on 40 000 random
points per mode; rounded keeps clear of the guard and every hole's reach and bridges the
gap; arcs holds the holes; the triangle's area and slope; swept hole entry = brute
force on 400 rays for both shapes; all 48 shape × rule combinations draw; URL keys
and the default preset. Renders: `node experiments/render-frames.js --mode=advance
--zone=rounded --holes=well --cards=round [--glass]`.

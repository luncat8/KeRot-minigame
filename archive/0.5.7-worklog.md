# 0.5.7 worklog — play again on a double click, the result card, and the review fixes

Draft: `0.5.7-draft.md` (the prompt and the reading of it).

## Review of the previous pass

`archive/0.5.6-worklog.md` rebuilt the fit view, moved the transport bar to the top of the
panel and split aiming from throwing. Its own validation used a stub DOM whose
`addEventListener` kept one handler per type, and that is where the review found the
biggest defect of the shipped page:

1. **The ☰ settings switch was bound twice.** `js/view.js` binds it to `toggle` (the
   drawer is View's), and `js/main.js` also called `UI.bindSettings()`, which bound
   `View.toggle` to the same button. In a browser both handlers fire, so one click opened
   the drawer and the second immediately closed it: the button looked dead in the fit
   view. The settings were still reachable by swiping the board left or by `?view=side`,
   which is why it read as working. `UI.bindSettings` is gone; View owns the switch.
2. **The keyboard listener stole keys from the panel.** `Space` and the arrows were
   `preventDefault`ed on `window` while a slider, select, radio or button had focus — so
   the ω/μ/mass sliders could not be arrowed, a checkbox could not be spaced, and the
   arrows nudged the stroke instead. Game keys are now ignored for events whose target is
   a `button`, `input`, `select` or `textarea` (Space and the arrows only; `R` and `Esc`
   still work everywhere).
3. **A paused board still read its keys.** Space queued a throw and the arrows nudged the
   stroke while paused; both now require `!paused`.
4. **Reset left the board paused.** `R`, the Reset button or (now) the play-again gesture
   dealt a new game that sat frozen with a "Resume" label. `reset()` clears `paused`.
5. **The spent HUD and panel lit the last thrower, not the winner**, and a tie was
   announced as a win for seat 1. The HUD cell, the panel row and the result line now agree
   with the scores; equal raw scores read as a draw, and a solo board is its seat's game.
6. **The ball dots spilled out of a cell at 7–8 balls** (`?balls=8`, the URL range allows
   it): their fixed 0.062 m step ran past the cell. Step and dot scale to the cell width.
7. **The aim label was drawn with the previous frame's text alignment** (canvas `start`,
   alphabetic): at the arrow tip it ran off along the board. It is centred on the tip now.

The stall/termination review found nothing to fix: 180 headless games over
balls 1–5 × seats 1–8 × three return rules all reached `scored`, with finite scores, a
valid winner and no board over `MAX_PUCKS`; a second fuzz that edited the roster, seat
types, votes, the speed rule and ω mid-game (153 edits, six seats) threw no exception and
left every puck's owner in range.

## The gesture

`js/game.js` `bindRestart(canvas)`, called from `bindInput` (the standalone page only —
`bindPointer` is shared with the Snowfall slot, which must answer once):

- a `pointerup` with a non-left button is ignored;
- the gesture is armed in `settle` and `scored`, i.e. from the last throw on;
- two taps within 600 ms whose client positions are within 40 px deal a new game
  (`reset()`); one tap, a far-apart pair and a slow pair do not.

A mouse double-click is that pair by definition; on touch it is a double tap, and
`touch-action: none` on the canvas (set by `bindPointer`) keeps the browser from turning
it into a zoom.

## The result card

- `index.html`: `#result` inside `#boardWrap`, hidden by default, holding a kicker, the
  winner line, the seat scores and the hint. `#boardWrap` is now `position: relative` in
  every layout; `#result` is absolute, `pointer-events: none`, and appears with a short
  scale/fade (off under `prefers-reduced-motion`). The hint names the gesture and `R`.
- `js/ui.js`: `syncResult()` hides the card outside `scored`, pins it to the canvas's
  `offsetLeft/Top/Width/Height` (so the side view's letterboxed board is covered exactly),
  and rewrites the winner/score line when the winner or a score changes. The panel's
  state line now reads `game over — … · double-click the board for a new game`.
- The help drawer and `README.md` describe the gesture.

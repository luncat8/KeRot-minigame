# 0.7.6 draft — user prompt

> inspect score for red and blue parts of big_disc.
>
> add settings to view and adjust curve.

Reading: the big disk's score map is the paint (js/field.js), and a seat is paid the
red channel of it (js/score.js). The board has a red part (field v > 0) and a blue
part (v < 0), and the paint's red channel over the field value — the pay curve, two
straight anchors as shipped — is the one function that decides what each part pays.

Two asks, one subject:

1. Inspect: show what the red and the blue part of the disk actually pay — on the
   disk itself (area shares and pay shares of the two parts) and live per seat
   (what a seat earns from each part right now, and what the finished board holds).
2. Settings: view the pay curve (a plot of the red channel over the field value, on
   a strip of the paint itself) and adjust it — a blue-endpoint slider and a bend
   knob — with paint and score moving together, as the page's rule "what is bright
   on the board is what pays" demands.

## Reading of the prompt

- **Presets** = named bundles of everything the panel controls: physics (ω, μk, μs,
  puck mass), disk pattern, return rule, colour scheme, and the table itself (seats,
  balls). Picking one from the panel applies the bundle and starts a fresh board —
  a preset is a way to *start over on purpose*, not a live tweak, so unlike the
  sliders it is allowed to reset the game.
- **URL parameters** are the boot-time share of the same thing: the same keys presets
  are made of, read from the query string before the first board is dealt, so a link
  like `?schema=rim6&seats=human,hunter,keeper&mass=8` opens the page already
  configured. A named `preset` parameter applies a bundle first, then the individual
  parameters override its keys. Bad or unknown parameters are ignored or clamped,
  never fatal — a link must always open. Picking a preset from the panel also writes
  its parameters into the URL (replaceState), so what you see is what you can share.
  With no parameters, boot is byte-identical to today.
- **The rim-schema problem** is a sampling problem, not an evaluation one: the
  predictor already knows exactly what a stroke does; the bots just never *try* the
  stroke the rim disk needs. A puck parked dead on the disk only locks within ~0.5 R,
  but the six lobes live at 0.78 R: a capture there needs the puck to arrive nearly
  co-rotating, i.e. along the tangent to the hold circle with residual speed ω·d.
  Aiming straight at a lobe's current position cannot work either way — the disk
  turns ~1 rad while the puck crosses the board. So: lead the aim by the flight
  time (the predictor knows the arrival), and add a fifth candidate group that
  throws the co-rotation tangent feed at the lobe that is *arriving*.
- **The competition balance** (random ≈ keeper/hunter since 0.3, before that hunter
  6.4x random) is diagnosed by measurement this time, not assumed: same game,
  same seeds, per-seat breakdowns of where the score actually comes from — rate
  held on the disk, time on the disk, locks, losses, returned balls — in uniform
  and mixed rosters, and only then a fix.
